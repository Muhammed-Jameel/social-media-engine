# Review, revisions, and owner calibration

Quality depends on both the model and actual production tools. A score is a documented
judgment, not a guarantee. Never populate passing reviews merely to unlock publishing.

## Review roles
Editorial and brand reviews are always required. Non-text content also requires visual
review. Arabic non-text content additionally requires Arabic typography review.
Each reviewer must be distinct from the producer. Use an independent reviewer/agent if
the host supports it within the user's permissions, or an actual human reviewer.
A single model must not invent separate identities to self-certify independence.
If independence is unavailable, save the draft and request human review.

Every review names the reviewer and current item hash, decision (pass/revise/reject),
specific observations and hard failures. Score eight dimensions 0-20:
concept, composition, typography, craft, brand, communication, polish, distinctiveness.
A passing role requires at least 145/160, no hard failures, and truthful required checks.
For text-only work, assess composition and typography as information structure and
readability, not imaginary visual assets.

Visual and Arabic reviewers must inspect the exact current asset hashes, at original
and mobile scales. Required passing checks: readability, dimensions, no_overflow,
rights, originality, sequence. Video additionally: full_video_watched, audio, decode.
Arabic additionally: shaping, rtl, line_breaks. Text captions also need language review.

## Revisions and approval
A failed criterion blocks approval. Revise, save a new item revision, then repeat reviews
against the NEW hash. All previous approvals are stale after content, assets, applicable
rules, or approved brand context change. A local prepared dispatch also freezes edits;
cancel that local intent first. Already-submitted content needs provider-side handling
and a new item for a corrected post, not local mutation of published history.

Show the owner actual media, final captions, platform variants, reviewer findings, and
remaining limitations. Only an explicit owner approval permits `item-approve`.
Content approval is NOT permission to publish. Exact destination/time confirmation is
a separate publishing step.

## First-run quality calibration
Generate 3-5 useful brand-specific samples, including a carousel and video when possible.
Collect 1-5 ratings for writing, visuals, brand accuracy, creativity, and motion plus
concrete comments. Distinguish one-post changes from reusable preferences.
Propose rules with explicit scopes and approve only what the owner confirms.
Regenerate at least one sample and explain the before/after differences.

Keep honest status: not reviewed, changes requested, approved for export, prepared for
publishing, provider accepted, or publication verified. Never substitute test-generated
scores or fabricated owner feedback for a genuine calibration session.
