# Strategy and editorial production

## Build a useful plan
Start from approved context, recent owner feedback, offers, audience needs, seasonal
timing, capacity, and evidence-backed performance. With no history, label hypotheses,
not fake benchmarks. Confirm the month and timezone.

Choose a small set of content pillars covering relevant objectives: awareness, useful
education, authority, trust, community, consideration, conversion. Do not force every
brand into identical ratios. Define a realistic cadence and one principal purpose per
post. Explain how the whole month helps the business, not just what to post each day.

Each brief needs: objective, audience, message, pillar, format, platform(s), language(s),
CTA, intended date, and expected audience response. Separate confirmed campaign facts
from proposals. Import the agreed plan; produce drafts linked to the plan ID.

## Writing quality
Develop a substantive idea before writing a hook. Research factual claims using primary
sources when available; retain URLs and relevant context. Do not fabricate statistics,
quotes, testimonials, results, product features, prices, certifications, or urgency.
Keep claims bounded to the evidence and the brand's claim policy.

Make copy specific, useful, natural, and readable. Avoid generic motivational padding,
empty superlatives, repeated hooks, and default emoji/hashtag formulas. Show why the
audience should care. CTA should follow from the post's purpose.

Adapt each variant deliberately:
- Instagram: visual-first story, helpful caption, save/share or relevant conversation.
- Facebook: context and community value, readable copy, meaningful links when suitable.
- TikTok: opening action/hook, spoken/natural pacing, shot sequence, captions and payoff.
- LinkedIn: professional substance without unsupported authority or artificial engagement.
- Other platforms: consult current format/capability guidance rather than guessing limits.

Language adaptation is not literal translation. Respect dialect, register, local context,
script direction, and audience knowledge. English, Arabic, Japanese, and other languages
are selected by the brand, not by README translations. Back-translate critical promises
when useful; get an appropriate human reviewer for high-stakes language decisions.

## Deliverables
An item includes the producer identity, distinct platform/language variants, caption,
format, asset IDs in viewing order, alt text per asset, adaptation rationale, sources,
verified claims when present, and a creative brief (idea, art direction, script as needed).
Use `context --item-file` to bind both brand and scoped rules before saving.
Never mark content approved just because the JSON saved successfully.
