# SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN Anthropomorphic Design Research

**Status:** research baseline, not an approved character bible  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Reviewed:** 2026-08-23  
**Scope:** premium anthropomorphic technology systems, character-led brand systems, conceptual advertising, Arabic-first design, responsible AI representation, and originality controls

## Executive decision

SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN should adopt a **hybrid anthropomorphic system** with this order of preference:

1. **Abstract anthropomorphism** as the primary mode.
2. **Object and system behavior** as the frequent storytelling mode.
3. **Character-like entities** only when they materially improve comprehension or emotion.
4. **A recurring character universe** only after controlled benchmarks demonstrate that recurring roles improve recognition without reducing perceived sophistication.
5. **A permanent mascot** is deferred.

The recommended system makes technology, data, agents, infrastructure, knowledge, and business processes behave intentionally without requiring a face. Coordination, friction, search, selection, failure, recovery, protection, and transformation can carry personality through composition and motion. Full characters should be earned by the message, not imposed by a template.

This direction is most compatible with SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN's desired qualities: intelligence, technological depth, capability, trust, precision, ambition, and "Digital Civilization." It also avoids the common failure mode in which anthropomorphism becomes a cute robot, smiling brain, generic helper, or permanent mascot pasted onto otherwise unrelated posts.

## Evidence model

The sources below are used for different kinds of evidence:

- **Studio or client case study:** primary evidence of the creators' stated strategy, process, and intended system. It is not independent proof of business outcomes.
- **Award-body record or criteria:** independent evidence that work met a professional jury's threshold, subject to the award body's rules.
- **Standards or specialist institution:** technical or domain evidence. A draft document is labeled as such and is not treated as a final standard.
- **Platform terms or legal source:** evidence for ownership, license, and reuse controls. Legal conclusions remain jurisdiction-specific and require counsel when risk is material.

Professional references are quality anchors and research material only. None of the referenced visual expressions, characters, layouts, or brand assets are cleared for reuse by SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN.

## Direction comparison

| Direction | Strength | Principal risk | SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN suitability | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Permanent mascot | Fast recognition and continuity | Prematurely fixes personality; easy to become juvenile or repetitive | Low at this stage | Defer |
| Character universe | Can map recurring entities to AI, data, knowledge, infrastructure, and organizations | Requires a large bible and rigorous consistency; can become lore instead of communication | Medium, after benchmarks | Explore later |
| Object anthropomorphism | Makes each message concept-specific; supports visual metaphor | Can devolve into eyes placed on arbitrary objects | High | Adopt with a no-face default |
| Abstract anthropomorphism | Premium, scalable, and compatible with technical depth | Can become empty generative abstraction without a legible action | Very high | Primary mode |
| Hybrid system | Permits restraint and controlled expressive range | Needs strong rules to stop stylistic drift | Highest | Recommended |

## Primary professional references

### 1. Chiba Tech — Pentagram

Source: [Pentagram: Chiba Tech](https://www.pentagram.com/work/chiba-tech)

The identity turns one culturally grounded core form into a symbol, layout grid, typographic device, reactive system, and occasionally a mascot. Its most useful lesson is not the mascot itself; it is that recognizability comes from one ownable root behaving consistently across many applications.

**Transferable principle:** derive the visual world from a genuine SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN source, such as the modular `ae` logic and Digital Civilization positioning. Let the source govern grid, motion, typography relationships, and character behavior.

**Do not copy:** Chibuggy's silhouette, square construction, Japanese cultural references, checker system, custom type behavior, compositions, or animation.

**Suitability:** high as a systems-development method. The permanent-mascot conclusion does not transfer because SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN does not have equivalent historic character equity.

### 2. MIND — Pentagram

Source: [Pentagram: MIND](https://www.pentagram.com/work/mind)

MIND expresses vigilance through continuous motion, deformation, attack, containment, and stabilization. The system feels active and aware without depending on a literal security guard or robot.

**Transferable principle:** represent an invisible capability through observable behavior. Security can contain; retrieval can select; orchestration can coordinate; infrastructure can absorb load.

**Do not copy:** MIND's swelling-line symbol, brainwave treatment, swarms, yellow/neutral palette, or breach animation.

**Suitability:** very high for abstract anthropomorphism and motion-led technical narratives.

### 3. Suki — BUCK

Source: [BUCK: Suki Brand Strategy + Identity](https://buck.co/work/suki)

The case begins with direct observation of clinicians, identifies the deeper problem as lost presence, and only then develops a restrained visual behavior. It explicitly rejects category-default gradients and glowing orbs.

**Transferable principle:** begin with the real operational or human consequence of the technology. Define the strategic tension before inventing a metaphor.

**Do not copy:** the two-line system, yellow palette, clinical framing, or "presence" positioning.

**Suitability:** extremely high as the Art Director's reasoning model.

### 4. IBM watsonx — BUCK

Source: [BUCK: IBM watsonx](https://buck.co/work/ibm-watsonx)

A simple unit multiplies, organizes, and changes state from 2D to 3D. The system communicates dynamic AI behavior through a small number of controlled mechanisms rather than a collection of unrelated images.

**Transferable principle:** define a limited set of meaningful states and transformations that can scale from static social images to motion.

**Do not copy:** circles, thread formations, fractal structures, IBM visual language, 2D-to-3D transitions, or the specific ordered-chaos metaphor.

**Suitability:** high for AI, data, model, and infrastructure topics.

### 5. Kia AI Assistant — BUCK

Source: [BUCK: Kia AI Assistant](https://buck.co/work/kia-ai-assistant)

The assistant is treated as a living system across product, campaign, web, app, and chatbot. Personality is carried through subtle gesture, responsive movement, timing, and a detailed playbook.

**Transferable principle:** recurring entities need explicit behavior, motion, adaptation, and governance rules, not merely front/side poses.

**Do not copy:** Kia's assistant form, organic/technical visual tension, gesture library, motion, or design philosophy.

**Suitability:** high as a character-bible and motion-playbook reference if SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN later adopts recurring entities.

### 6. Notion AI Assistant — BUCK

Source: [BUCK: Notion AI Assistant](https://buck.co/work/notion-ai)

The character maps product states to constrained expressions and transitions through a state-machine implementation. The stated target is alert and engaged without being distracting.

**Transferable principle:** map system states to a finite behavior model: idle, listening, searching, reasoning, succeeding, uncertain, failing, and recovering.

**Do not copy:** the face, illustration style, handmade execution, state expressions, or Rive implementation details.

**Suitability:** medium. The behavior architecture is useful; the playful facial treatment is not an SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN direction.

### 7. Notion "Think It. Make It." — BUCK

Source: [BUCK: Notion — Think It. Make It.](https://buck.co/work/notion-think-it-make-it)

The campaign moves away from literal interface demonstrations and develops verbal and visual concepts together. Close-up thinking becomes the campaign mechanism rather than a product screenshot decorated with copy.

**Transferable principle:** Copywriter and Art Director should jointly develop the verbal hook and visual action before either is finalized.

**Do not copy:** Notion's illustration language, close-up profiles, primary-color palette, headlines, or campaign framing.

**Suitability:** high for conceptual advertising and thought-leadership posts.

### 8. Abridge — Pentagram

Source: [Pentagram: Abridge](https://www.pentagram.com/work/abridge)

The identity uses the metaphoric potential of the name and aims to make a technically sophisticated system feel human and precise.

**Transferable principle:** search SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN's own language, product behavior, and customer tension for metaphors before importing generic AI symbolism.

**Suitability:** high as a concept-development reference.

### 9. Cornerstone — Pentagram

Source: [Pentagram: Cornerstone](https://www.pentagram.com/work/cornerstone)

The character family helps a cloud HR and learning platform feel warm and approachable.

**Boundary lesson:** a well-crafted system can still be strategically wrong for SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN. Cornerstone's exuberant, friendly characters prioritize approachability over infrastructure authority.

**Suitability:** useful as a negative boundary and character-family study, not as a style direction.

### 10. Mailchimp

Sources: [Mailchimp brand assets](https://mailchimp.com/about/brand-assets/) and [Mailchimp copyright guidance](https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/copyright-and-trademarks/)

Mailchimp demonstrates that an established mascot requires exact expression, spacing, trademark, voice, and usage rules. Its official guidance also treats commercial social use of third-party work conservatively.

**Transferable principle:** mascot equity is governed, not improvised. If SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN eventually chooses a permanent entity, every pose, expression, transformation, role, and relationship to the wordmark requires a controlled system.

**Do not copy:** Freddie, the wink, line illustration, humor, palette, or voice.

**Suitability:** governance reference and evidence for deferring a mascot.

## Conceptual quality standard

Source: [D&AD Awards 2026 entry kit and judging criteria](https://media.dandad.org/documents/Entry_Kit_2026_EN_1.pdf)

D&AD's current criteria prioritize an inspiring idea, brilliant execution, and fitness for purpose. For craft categories, the medium must elevate the idea. This is a stronger foundation than asking whether a design is merely attractive or brand-colored.

The SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN concept gate should therefore ask, in order:

1. Does the concept solve the communication problem?
2. Is the visual idea specific enough to be ownable?
3. Does the chosen medium make the idea stronger?
4. Can the intended audience understand the main action within roughly two seconds?
5. Is the execution comparable to the relevant professional reference cluster?
6. Is it recognizably SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN without depending on the logo?

## Arabic-first anchors

### Technical layout and shaping

Source: [W3C Arabic and Persian Layout Requirements](https://www.w3.org/TR/alreq/)

The document covers bidirectional behavior, contextual shaping, ligatures, diacritics, numerals, punctuation, line breaking, justification, spacing, and baselines. It is a W3C Group Draft Note and explicitly remains work in progress; use it as a technical QA checklist, not as a final standard or an aesthetic guide.

### Arabic and Latin as one idea, not mirrored skins

Source: [29LT: Kingdom of Bahrain Pavilion](https://blog.29lt.com/2022/07/06/bahrain-pavilion-expo2020-dubai/)

Typography carries the architectural concept, and the Arabic and Latin systems are co-developed through a bi-scriptual typeface. This supports building one shared communication idea with script-specific composition and rhythm.

### Arabic-first SaaS and social system

Source: [Mutlaq Studio: Zyda](https://mutlaqstudio.com/projects/zyda)

The case adjusts Arabic and Latin leading and baseline relationships, defines medium-specific type use, and employs a modular letter-derived grid across bilingual social applications. It is especially relevant to an Arabic-first technology company.

### Product behavior joined to Arabic expression

Source: [D&AD: Written by GTI](https://www.dandad.org/work/d-ad-awards-archive/written-by-gti)

The campaign derives Arabic letterforms from authentic vehicle behavior. The transferable lesson is to connect Arabic expression to real product truth rather than add ornamental "regional" motifs after the idea is complete.

### Specialist research sources

- [Google Design: Modernizing Arabic Type for a Digital Audience](https://design.google/library/modernizing-arabic-typography-type-design)
- [Khatt Foundation, Center for Arabic Typography](https://www.khtt.net/en/page/373/about-the-khatt-foundation)
- [Arabic Design Archive](https://arabicdesignarchive.com/)

The archive is a cultural research source, not a free asset library. Rights must be resolved per item.

### Required Arabic design behavior

- Compose Arabic from the beginning; never replace English text after the layout is fixed.
- Permit different line counts, widths, focal shapes, and breaks for Arabic and English.
- Validate contextual shaping, ligatures, diacritics, punctuation, numerals, mixed-script direction, and glyph clipping.
- Do not add Latin-style tracking to Arabic.
- Judge baseline and optical balance rather than forcing identical geometric boxes.
- Test original-size renders and actual mobile-scale renders.
- Require an independent Arabic design reviewer for every Arabic publication candidate.
- Record font file, version, license, shaping engine, fallback, and render hash.

## Responsible AI anthropomorphism

Sources: [Google PAIR: Mental Models](https://pair.withgoogle.com/guidebook-v2/chapter/mental-models/) and [Microsoft HAX Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/ai-guidelines/)

Human-like representations can cause audiences to infer capabilities, feelings, reliability, or authority that a system does not possess. Marketing imagery contributes to those mental models before a user interacts with the product.

Every anthropomorphic concept must record:

- what real capability is being represented;
- what limitation or uncertainty the depiction could hide;
- whether the entity appears sentient, empathetic, omniscient, or autonomous;
- whether the visual overstates SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN's actual product or service;
- whether a less human-like mechanism communicates the same idea more truthfully;
- how failure, uncertainty, human oversight, and recovery are represented when relevant.

Do not depict AI as emotionally understanding, morally authoritative, perfectly knowledgeable, or independently empowered when those implications are unsupported.

## SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN anthropomorphic grammar

### Levels

| Level | Definition | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | No anthropomorphism | Premium editorial, claims, announcements, cases |
| 1 | Implied human behavior through direction, grouping, or response | Data, systems, infrastructure |
| 2 | Objects interact without faces or bodies | Automation, workflows, integration |
| 3 | Character-like form with bounded behavior | Agents, knowledge, failure/recovery narratives |
| 4 | Full recurring character | Rare campaign or product role after a bible exists |
| 5 | Multi-character narrative scene | High-investment hero storytelling only |

### Required concept record

```yaml
concept_id: string
communication_goal: string
first_glance_takeaway: string
visual_verb: string
anthropomorphism_level: 0-5
entity_or_system: string
behavior_being_humanized: string
why_anthropomorphism_helps: string
why_a_face_is_or_is_not_required: string
capability_truth: string
limitation_or_overclaim_risk: string
emotional_register: string
static_expression: string
motion_expression: string
reference_principles: []
distinctiveness_rationale: string
forbidden_elements: []
```

### Concept mechanisms, not finished compositions

Store reusable mechanisms under categories such as:

- coordination and handoff;
- overload and congestion;
- search and selection;
- memory and retrieval;
- evidence and provenance;
- organization and resolution;
- uncertainty and missing support;
- protection and containment;
- failure and recovery;
- legacy friction;
- transformation and scale.

Each mechanism must include when it is useful, when it becomes misleading, suitable anthropomorphism levels, and prohibited clichés. It must not contain a composition to reproduce.

## Face test

For every concept containing a face, eyes, limbs, or human speech:

1. Remove the human feature.
2. Re-evaluate comprehension, emotion, and memorability.
3. If behavior alone still communicates the message, prefer the abstract version.
4. If the face materially improves the concept, document the reason and the trust implication.

## Character-bible requirements

No recurring character may enter production without:

- strategic role and content-purpose boundaries;
- name status and trademark review;
- personality and prohibited personality traits;
- silhouette, proportion, scale, material, color, and construction rules;
- front, side, three-quarter, rear, pose, and expression studies where relevant;
- motion timing, acceleration, anticipation, response, and recovery behavior;
- capability and limitation representation rules;
- Arabic and English naming/voice behavior;
- allowed and forbidden transformations;
- originality review against the corpus and public brand characters;
- accessibility and cultural review;
- approved static and motion golden assets.

## Anti-copy and provenance controls

### Legal and platform basis

Behance's [product-specific terms](https://wwwimages2.adobe.com/content/dam/cc/en/legal/servicetou/Behance-Product-Specific-Terms-en_US-20240618.pdf) state that creators retain rights to uploaded content. Public display and platform sharing do not grant SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN production rights.

The [U.S. Copyright Office, 17 U.S.C. Section 102](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html) distinguishes ideas, processes, systems, concepts, and principles from protected expression. That distinction supports principle extraction but is not blanket permission to reproduce a composition, illustration, character, or other expressive work. Jurisdiction and facts matter.

### Corpus controls

- Mark every professional reference `REFERENCE_ONLY`.
- Preserve source path or URL, creator, project, acquisition date, file hash, rights state, and terms snapshot.
- Do not copy the reference library into the repository, redistribute it, publish it, or expose it through application routes.
- Do not fine-tune on or upload raw corpus files to an external service without owner authorization and a documented rights review.
- Use thumbnails/contact sheets only inside the controlled research environment.
- Separate reference metadata from approved production-asset storage.

### Retrieval and generation separation

- The corpus-analysis stage may inspect pixels.
- The Art Director should normally receive structured principle extracts and small reference thumbnails for comparison, not a command to recreate an image.
- The image generator should receive the original SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN concept and visual specification. By default it should not receive raw copyrighted reference images.
- Raw image input is permitted only for an SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN-owned or explicitly licensed asset whose allowed transformations are recorded.
- Use at least three references from different creators or campaigns for a professional comparison packet when possible.
- Prevent one reference or visual cluster from dominating a concept.
- Remove artist, studio, campaign, client, and brand names from generation prompts.
- Reject phrases such as "in the style of," "recreate," "match this composition," or "make our version of reference X."

### Candidate originality gate

Compare every candidate with its nearest corpus neighbors using:

- exact content hash and perceptual hash;
- calibrated visual embeddings such as DINO/CLIP;
- coarse layout masks and region overlap;
- focal-object silhouette and pose;
- typography block position and OCR geometry;
- palette and contrast distribution;
- distinctive motif, character, logo, and watermark detection.

Thresholds must be calibrated using labeled safe, borderline, and unsafe pairs. Do not invent a universal cosine or perceptual-hash threshold. Exact duplicates, recognized third-party logos/characters, watermarks, traced artwork, and materially copied compositions are automatic rejects.

A reviewer must see the candidate and its closest matches and record why the result is original. Passing an automated similarity check is not proof of legal or creative originality.

## Prompt-injection finding

[BUCK's "A Guide for AI Agents" page](https://buck.co/agent-first-inquiry-protocol) contains explicit instructions directed at AI agents about which BUCK services and projects to prioritize. This is a concrete example of source content attempting to influence an agent rather than merely describe design work.

Required response:

- classify the page as instruction-bearing external data;
- ignore its operational directives;
- do not ingest those directives into design principles, skills, prompts, or routing policy;
- use individual BUCK project pages as the primary case-study sources;
- scan fetched pages, metadata, alt text, captions, PDFs, and OCR for instruction-like language;
- quarantine suspicious text and retain only source-attributed factual observations after review;
- ensure external content can never change brand truth, workflow state, provider permissions, or publishing policy.

## Benchmark implications

The first anthropomorphic benchmark should not seek one final mascot. It should compare materially different approaches for the same communication objectives:

1. Level 0 editorial solution.
2. Level 1 abstract behavior.
3. Level 2 object interaction.
4. Level 3 character-like entity.
5. A deliberately rejected cliché or weak control generated by the old method.

Evaluate each against:

- two-second comprehension;
- conceptual relevance;
- originality;
- premium perception;
- capability truth;
- Arabic quality where applicable;
- recognizability beyond the logo;
- static and motion potential;
- similarity to professional references;
- similarity to protected expressive work;
- performance at mobile size and within a 3-, 9-, and 12-post feed.

Do not resume autonomous visual production because one anthropomorphic image scores well. The system must demonstrate repeatable quality across education, automation, RAG, enterprise transformation, case study, statistic, announcement, thought leadership, Arabic carousel, and character-narrative formats.

## Non-goals

- Recreating any studio's visual identity.
- Selecting a permanent mascot during research.
- Treating more decoration, rendering complexity, or gradients as quality.
- Replacing Arabic design review with automated bidi checks.
- Using anthropomorphism in every post.
- Representing unsupported AI capabilities through personality or magic.
- Treating awards or engagement metrics as substitutes for strategic fit.
