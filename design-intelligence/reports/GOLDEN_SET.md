## Read this distinction first

“Golden set” refers to two different things and they must never be conflated.

1. **Professional reference anchors** are third-party, reference-only examples used to calibrate concept, hierarchy, craft, Arabic, systems thinking, or failure boundaries. They are not SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN assets, are not cleared for reuse, and do not become training or generation material.
2. **SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN golden outputs** are original, rights-cleared, hash-bound SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN assets that have passed every professional critic, comparison, originality, Arabic, feed, and owner gate.

Only the second category can become regression fixtures for the production engine. It is currently empty.

## SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN admission contract

An output enters `APPROVED_GOLD` only when all of the following are true.

### Ownership and provenance

- The visual and verbal concept is original to SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN.
- Every asset is owned, generated under recorded terms, or explicitly licensed for the use.
- Product UI, data, claims, people, fonts, marks, and generated assets have source and rights evidence.
- No third-party reference pixels or graphics appear in the production asset.
- Nearest-reference originality review records independent derivation.

### Design and communication

- The asset executes a message-specific idea and two-second takeaway.
- The image and words complete one another.
- The composition, type, imagery, material, light, and micro-detail follow a coherent rationale.
- Arabic assets pass every rule in [ARABIC_DESIGN_RULES.md](./ARABIC_DESIGN_RULES.md).
- Anthropomorphic assets pass the level, face, capability, and originality controls in [SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN_ANTHROPOMORPHIC_SYSTEM.md](./SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN_ANTHROPOMORPHIC_SYSTEM.md).
- The work remains recognizably SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN with the logo hidden.

### Pixel-grounded critics

- Technical preflight passes.
- Senior Art Director, Senior Graphic Designer, and Social Performance Strategist inspect the exact current original and mobile pixels.
- An Arabic Design Reviewer also inspects every Arabic asset.
- Every required critic independently scores at least `145/160`.
- Every required critic records no hard fail.
- Every required critic judges the candidate `comparable` or `above` the relevant professional anchors.
- Critiques contain region-specific evidence and are bound to the current SHA-256.

### Comparative proof

- The candidate wins its concept tournament.
- It decisively beats the old engine on the identical brief in a blind or semi-blind comparison.
- It is not merely the best of weak candidates; `tie-neither-professional` is resolved by restart.
- It passes nearest-corpus and recent-feed originality/repetition review.
- It remains strong inside `3`, `9`, and `12`-post simulations.

### Governance

- Every release blocker in [CREATIVE_FAILURE_ANALYSIS.md](./CREATIVE_FAILURE_ANALYSIS.md#explicit-release-blockers) is closed with durable evidence.
- Owner approval is separate from creative approval and bound to the exact current asset hash.
- The approved artifact and all evidence are immutable or content-addressed.

## Invalidation and regression

Gold approval is invalidated for the changed asset when any of these changes:

- raster pixels or dimensions;
- copy or authored line breaks;
- font file or weight;
- focal asset, UI, data, logo, or license state;
- composition or color behavior;
- output encoder when it changes visible pixels;
- active brand version.

A knowledge-base or engine change does not rewrite historic gold. Instead, rerun the controlled briefs and compare new outputs with the immutable approved assets. A new system passes regression only when it preserves or improves:

- professional critic outcomes;
- professional-anchor comparability;
- Arabic correctness;
- originality;
- feed diversity;
- old-vs-new preference.

Engagement data may inform future hypotheses, but performance cannot downgrade the professional, rights, Arabic, or brand gates.

## Promotion procedure

1. Complete the ten-type benchmark with multiple concepts per type.
2. Reject weak concepts and iterate only conceptually sound candidates.
3. Inspect final original, mobile, and feed pixels.
4. Complete all independent current-hash critiques and pairwise comparisons.
5. Close system-level release blockers; do not waive them for a visually strong exception.
6. Submit qualifying assets for separate owner approval.
7. Add only approved hashes to the SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN registry.
8. Use the immutable set for future creative-engine regression.

Until this procedure produces at least one qualifying item—and the broader benchmark proves repeatability—the accurate statement is: **professional reference anchors exist; the SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN golden set does not yet.**
