# SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN Anthropomorphic System

**Status:** prototype; no permanent mascot approved  
**Recommendation:** hybrid system  
**Default range:** levels `0–3`, with levels `1–3` preferred when anthropomorphism adds value  
**Production decision:** benchmark use only until the release gates pass

## Decision

SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN will humanize **behavior before appearance**.

The system begins with abstract movement, object interaction, and bounded character-like behavior. It does not begin with a face, cute robot, or recurring mascot. This lets SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN make retrieval, orchestration, memory, evidence, overload, protection, failure, and recovery emotionally legible while preserving enterprise authority.

The adopted order is:

1. abstract anthropomorphism as the primary mode;
2. object and system behavior as the frequent storytelling mode;
3. character-like entities only when they improve comprehension or emotional relevance;
4. a recurring character universe only after controlled evidence supports it;
5. a permanent mascot deferred.

This document operationalizes [character-system.yaml](../social-media-plugin/character-system.yaml), [anthropomorphism.yaml](../principles/anthropomorphism.yaml), and the research in [ANTHROPOMORPHIC_RESEARCH.md](./ANTHROPOMORPHIC_RESEARCH.md).

## What premium anthropomorphism means

Premium anthropomorphism gives a real system an observable intention, effort, response, limitation, or relationship. It is not a layer of personality pasted onto a generic composition.

Good questions are:

- What is trying to coordinate, retrieve, protect, decide, or recover?
- What resistance or uncertainty can the viewer see?
- Which capability is real?
- Where does evidence, permission, or human judgment enter?
- Would the same story remain understandable without a face?

The target emotional range is alert, precise, capable, calm, curious, determined, or cautiously relieved. Constant cheerfulness, broad cartoon comedy, or exaggerated cuteness is outside the default system.

## Level system

| Level | Definition | Typical SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN use | Control |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 0 | No anthropomorphism | Editorial statements, announcements, statistics, product proof, solemn topics | Preserve feed seriousness and evidence authority |
| 1 | Human behavior implied through path, pause, alignment, grouping, response, or effort | Data, infrastructure, retrieval, uncertainty, system state | The action must remain legible without anatomy |
| 2 | Objects interact or coordinate without character anatomy | Automation, workflows, handoffs, integration, protection | No arbitrary eyes, limbs, or speech |
| 3 | A character-like form communicates through silhouette, gesture, or pose; a face is normally unnecessary | Agents, memory, failure/recovery, selected education | Require a capability boundary and face-removal result |
| 4 | Full character with controlled expression | Rare, high-value campaign role after a character bible | Requires approved recurrence studies and originality review |
| 5 | Multi-actor narrative scene with environment and consequence | Exceptional hero storytelling only | Requires substantial craft, cultural review, and measurable comprehension benefit |

The level is chosen per message. It must vary across a feed. Anthropomorphism is never a permanent slot in the layout.

## Concept mechanisms

These are mechanisms for original ideation, not compositions to reproduce.

| Category | Observable behaviors | Useful for | Principal risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coordination | handoff, synchronized lift, shared path, assembly | Agents, automation, integrated operations | Generic teamwork metaphor |
| Overload | congestion, compression, queue, dropped load | Legacy systems, operational debt, capacity | Comic distress that trivializes the problem |
| Discovery | search, reveal, selection, excavation | RAG, research, knowledge retrieval | Omniscient “magic answer” implication |
| Organization | sorting, alignment, containment, indexing | Data, workflows, operating systems | Decorative grids with no before/after state |
| Uncertainty | missing piece, unstable path, incomplete map, confidence boundary | Hallucination, governance, decision support | Presenting uncertainty as incompetence or panic |
| Memory | archive, retrieval, association, decay and recovery | Knowledge systems, RAG, institutional memory | Literal brain imagery |
| Protection | boundary, escort, filtration, quarantine | Security, privacy, governance | Implied perfect safety |
| Failure | stall, drift, collision, graceful fallback | Reliability, legacy modernization, human handoff | Slapstick failure or fear marketing |
| Scale | controlled replication, widening channel, coordinated expansion | Growth, infrastructure, transformation | Empty multiplication of shapes |
| Transformation | chaos to order, isolated to connected, opaque to legible | Enterprise change, automation, system integration | Generic “before/after” tidying |

For each concept, the art director must derive a new actor, setting, state, and visual relationship from the communication problem. The table does not authorize reusing a prior scene.

## Selection procedure

### 1. Establish the real behavior

Name the actual capability and consequence. “AI thinks” is not sufficient. “The retrieval layer selects source-backed evidence before an answer is composed” is concrete enough to visualize.

### 2. Build a level-0 control

Sketch an editorial or product-proof solution without anthropomorphism. This prevents character use from being assumed.

### 3. Test levels 1–3

Explore materially different behavior-led solutions. The best level is the least human-like treatment that preserves comprehension, emotional relevance, and memorability.

### 4. Run the face-removal test

For any eyes, mouth, limbs, or speech:

1. remove the feature;
2. compare comprehension and emotion;
3. inspect whether pose, state, and interaction still carry the idea;
4. keep the feature only if it creates a documented benefit that behavior cannot provide;
5. record the trust implication.

If the concept collapses without eyes, return to the behavior, pose, or relationship rather than adding more expression.

### 5. Compare with the content and feed

Reject the anthropomorphic direction if it weakens seriousness, repeats a recent level or silhouette, dominates the message, or looks less credible beside the level-0 control.

## Visual construction rules

### Silhouette and proportion

An entity must remain identifiable as a clean silhouette at mobile size. Proportions should communicate role and state through weight, balance, reach, tension, or containment. Default forms should avoid oversized heads, tiny bodies, soft toy proportions, and generic capsule robots.

### Material

Entities belong to SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN's material world: paper/mineral composites, machined surfaces, glass, ceramic, or brushed metal with controlled green activation. Material choice must explain function or atmosphere. A recurring entity cannot change material logic merely to fit a post.

### Gesture and pose

Pose carries the primary emotion. Use lean, compression, distance, orientation, contact, grouping, and tempo before facial expression. Gestures should embody the operational verb rather than mime a generic “helpful assistant.”

### Face and anatomy

No face is the default. If a face is approved, it must have a constrained feature system, expression range, and capability implication. Eyes added to an arbitrary object, white-glove limbs, floating robot heads, human speech bubbles, and face-first cuteness are automatic rejection signals.

### Light and state

Light reveals material and operational state. A local pale-neon charge may identify selection, activation, evidence, or resolved flow. It must not function as a halo that makes the entity seem magical or sentient.

### Environment and depth

The environment provides consequence and scale. Foreground states the stakes, the action plane carries behavior, and the background supplies context or evidence. Character and environment must share perspective, contact, shadow, occlusion, and light.

### Motion

Motion has anticipation, action, and settled state. Timing can communicate caution, confidence, uncertainty, overload, or recovery. Perpetual bobbing, blinking, particle trails, and decorative idle loops do not count as behavior.

## Capability ethics

Anthropomorphic imagery shapes audience expectations before product use. Every concept must state:

- the real capability represented;
- the limitation or uncertainty the depiction could hide;
- whether human approval, evidence, permission, or handoff matters;
- whether the entity appears sentient, empathetic, omniscient, morally authoritative, or fully autonomous;
- whether the outcome is guaranteed or merely supported;
- the less human-like alternative considered.

Prohibited implications include sentience, perfect knowledge, emotional understanding, autonomous authority, and guaranteed outcomes when they are not supported. Show confidence boundaries, source boundaries, approvals, recoverable failure, or human handoff when relevant.

## Arabic and cultural behavior

Anthropomorphic scenes do not make Arabic a caption layer. Arabic is authored before composition, and its RTL entry helps determine gaze, movement, and counterweight. Do not mirror an LTR character scene mechanically.

For Arabic work:

- protect the optical right edge and the main Arabic headline field;
- keep Arabic joins and diacritics free from busy materials or green spill;
- treat Latin product names and numerals as controlled directional islands;
- avoid imported cultural motifs, costumes, humor, or gestures unless the concept requires them and a reviewer validates them;
- require an independent Arabic design reviewer to inspect the exact current pixels.

The full typographic rules are in [ARABIC_DESIGN_RULES.md](./ARABIC_DESIGN_RULES.md).

## Recurring-entity gate

No recurring entity may enter normal production without all of the following:

- strategic role and content-purpose boundaries;
- name status and trademark review where relevant;
- personality and prohibited personality traits;
- silhouette, proportion, scale, construction, material, and color rules;
- front, side, three-quarter, and rear views where needed;
- pose vocabulary and expression range;
- lighting response and allowed environmental states;
- motion timing, anticipation, response, and recovery behavior;
- capability and limitation representation rules;
- Arabic and English naming or voice behavior;
- allowed and forbidden transformations;
- accessibility and cultural review;
- originality comparison against the nearest corpus and public character systems;
- approved static and motion assets bound to exact hashes;
- evidence that recurrence improves recognition and maturity across the controlled benchmark.

Until that gate passes, all entities are prototypes and must not accumulate informal canon.

## Anti-copy and provenance

Professional characters and campaigns are reference-only. Do not reproduce their silhouette, proportions, eyes, limbs, material treatment, signature pose, behavior sequence, setting, or motion timing. Do not include studio, artist, campaign, or character names in image-generation prompts.

Generation may receive:

- the original SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN communication problem;
- an original behavior and scene specification;
- extracted principles with explicit `mustNotCopy` boundaries;
- SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN-owned or explicitly licensed assets with recorded transformation rights.

Generation may not receive raw corpus pixels by default. Final candidates require a reviewer to compare silhouette, pose, materials, focal layout, OCR geometry, and distinctive motifs against nearest references. An automated similarity pass is not proof of originality or legal clearance.

## Automatic rejection

Reject immediately when:

- anthropomorphism is not needed by the communication;
- a cute face or generic robot carries the entire idea;
- the level weakens enterprise authority;
- the entity implies unsupported sentience or capability;
- character anatomy, contact, perspective, or light is visibly broken;
- a recurring entity is inconsistent with its approved studies;
- the candidate resembles a protected professional character or campaign too closely;
- the same face, pose, or entity is forced into unrelated posts;
- Arabic type is treated as an overlay or becomes unreadable;
- the story cannot be explained as an observable behavior and consequence.

The enforceable pattern set is [forbidden-patterns.yaml](../social-media-plugin/forbidden-patterns.yaml).

## Benchmark before adoption

For the same objectives, compare:

1. a level-0 editorial or product-proof solution;
2. a level-1 abstract-behavior solution;
3. a level-2 object-interaction solution;
4. a level-3 character-like solution;
5. an intentionally weak old-method or cliché control.

Judge two-second comprehension, relevance, originality, premium perception, capability truth, Arabic quality, brand recognition without the logo, mobile rendering, professional-anchor comparison, and `3/9/12`-post feed behavior.

One strong character image is not enough. The system must prove flexible quality across education, automation, RAG, enterprise transformation, case study, statistic, announcement, thought leadership, Arabic carousel, and character narrative. No SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN anthropomorphic output is approved as gold today; the admission standard is in [GOLDEN_SET.md](./GOLDEN_SET.md).
