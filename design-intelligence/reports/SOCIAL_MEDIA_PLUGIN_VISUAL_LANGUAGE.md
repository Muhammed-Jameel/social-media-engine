# SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN Visual Language

**Status:** experimental, benchmark-only  
**Brand version:** `final-2026.1`  
**Visual-world name:** **Operational Intelligence**  
**Production decision:** normal visual generation remains blocked until the benchmark and release gates in [CREATIVE_FAILURE_ANALYSIS.md](./CREATIVE_FAILURE_ANALYSIS.md) pass.

## The visual idea

SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN makes invisible systems visible as **physical behaviors inside a precise, calm, material world**.

Strategy, data, agents, retrieval, infrastructure, and transformation should not appear as generic technology decoration. They should organize, retrieve, connect, hand off, protect, resolve, recover, or scale in a way the viewer can understand within roughly two seconds.

The recognition test is stricter than palette compliance:

> With the logo hidden, does the work still feel like SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN through its operational story, Arabic authority, material light, controlled green charge, and precise relationships?

If recognition depends mainly on the logo, green background, rounded cards, or a systems-line motif, the design is not yet distinctive.

The machine-readable source of truth is [visual-grammar.yaml](../social-media-plugin/visual-grammar.yaml). The principles below interpret that grammar; they do not replace it.

## Creative posture

SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN should feel:

- intelligent rather than mystical;
- capable rather than omnipotent;
- infrastructural rather than gadget-like;
- ambitious rather than loud;
- humanly legible rather than cute;
- regionally authoritative rather than ornamentally “Arabic”;
- exact rather than sterile;
- premium through judgment and craft, not visual excess.

The desired blend is sophisticated editorial advertising, premium technology branding, conceptual imagery, product truth, and selective intelligent anthropomorphism.

## Non-negotiable design logic

### One message-specific idea

Every post needs a single visual proposition, not a style label. “Premium,” “editorial,” “AI,” and “modern” are not concepts. The brief must name:

1. the audience tension;
2. the two-second takeaway;
3. the visual verb;
4. the focal object, actor, or typographic phrase;
5. the visible state change, evidence, or consequence;
6. how the image and words complete one another.

A post can be extremely simple. One excellent image, object, line, or phrase is preferable to a tray of explanatory icons.

### Three-beat first read

The opening scan should contain no more than:

1. the focal idea;
2. a clarifying or evidentiary beat;
3. a quiet brand or action signal.

More information may reward a second look, but it must not compete with the opening scan. Evidence is subordinate, not hidden. The logo signs the communication after the world has already established identity.

### Relationships before components

Every major decision must describe a relationship:

- headline ↔ image: insight and proof, not caption and illustration;
- subject ↔ negative space: tension, direction, absence, or a protected text field;
- foreground ↔ background: stakes, action, and context;
- accent color ↔ state: activation, resolution, evidence, or warning;
- crop ↔ meaning: scale, continuation, urgency, or movement;
- texture ↔ atmosphere: material or evidence, never generic noise;
- UI ↔ claim: real proof, never floating technology scenery.

Elements that cannot name a semantic, hierarchy, framing, evidence, or rhythm role should be removed.

## Composition grammar

### Focal dominance

One meaning-bearing object, actor, scene, or phrase must carry roughly half the perceived visual weight. The exact ratio is optical, not a coordinate prescription. Secondary content must support the focal point instead of creating equal-weight islands.

### Asymmetric counterweight

Off-center focal mass is normally balanced by purposeful space or a smaller evidence block. Arabic compositions should begin from the right-side reading entry, but the image may pull leftward, diagonally, or into depth when that movement supports the story. Mirroring a Latin layout is prohibited.

### Semantic depth

When the concept benefits from dimensionality:

- **foreground:** names the cost, stakes, obstruction, or immediate evidence;
- **action plane:** carries the operational behavior or transformation;
- **background:** supplies context, source, scale, or a verifiable end state.

Flat design remains valid when type or a minimal object is conceptually strong enough. Flatness by default is not restraint.

### Purposeful crop

Crop only when the continuation outside the frame adds scale, energy, intimacy, or a sense of a larger world. Do not crop Arabic letterforms, faces, proof-bearing UI, or critical product detail.

### Negative space with a declared job

Large empty regions must act as one of the following:

- headline field;
- absence metaphor;
- gaze or motion destination;
- counterweight;
- feed-level breathing beat.

Unoccupied canvas is not automatically premium negative space.

### Grid behavior

A six-column base is a useful starting discipline. A concept may bend the grid once when the break is named and justified. The grid controls relationships, not a permanent set of coordinates. Repeating a frame–header–headline–rail–footer skeleton is prohibited.

## Typography

### Display typography is part of the image

Headline shape, line length, counter-space, and scale must be developed with the visual concept. Type should interlock with, counterbalance, contain, reveal, or direct the imagery rather than label an already completed picture.

Use steep scale contrast. A perceived display-to-support ratio around `3:1` is a starting hypothesis, not a fixed formula. Supporting text gets a deliberate measure and generous leading; excess copy moves to another slide or the caption rather than shrinking into a disclaimer.

Use one emphasis method per post—weight, scale, color, or position. Neon-green pills, underlines, and multiple highlight treatments should not accumulate.

### Production fonts

- Latin display: **Dh Ranclo Bold**, only when the exact approved production file and weight have loaded.
- Arabic display: **Ghroob Arabic ITF**, only when the exact approved production file and weight have loaded.

A family name in SVG, CSS, or provider metadata is not proof. Each final raster must be bound to loaded font-file hashes and inspected at original and mobile sizes. Font substitution, synthetic weight, broken joins, or export-time clipping blocks release. See [ARABIC_DESIGN_RULES.md](./ARABIC_DESIGN_RULES.md).

## Color behavior

| Role | Active values | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep field | `#003F35` | Authority, infrastructure, calm depth |
| Paper field | `#F4F8F5` | Clarity, editorial breath, evidence |
| Depth range | `#00221D`, `#00302A`, `#0B594A`, `#CFDDD4` | Planes, material separation, quiet states |
| Active charge | `#0EDB23`, `#77FF70` | Intelligence acting, resolved flow, selected evidence |

Neon is scarce and semantic. It identifies an active relationship or state; it is not a default fill, glow, or outline. Non-neutral colors require a recorded meaning. Material response and local light come before bloom.

Across a feed, light, dark, restrained, and higher-energy fields must be scheduled intentionally. A nine-post view dominated by the same green field or neon intensity fails the rhythm gate.

## Imagery and the material world

Preferred modes, selected according to message:

1. conceptual photomanipulation;
2. bespoke 3D;
3. conceptual illustration;
4. owned product UI used as proof;
5. typography-led composition;
6. abstract system behavior when the action remains legible.

The focal asset should be approved before final layout. A weak image cannot be rescued by cards, gradients, or brand furniture.

### Material vocabulary

Use one coherent material story per direction. Current allowed vocabulary includes:

- restrained mineral grain;
- paper fiber;
- precise machined surfaces;
- glass;
- ceramic;
- brushed metal.

Materials must behave consistently under light. Contacts, masks, occlusion, perspective, shadows, reflections, depth of field, and transitions are inspected at original resolution. Texture must support atmosphere, era, evidence, or function; a generic noise overlay is prohibited.

### Light

- **Light mode:** soft Baghdad daylight filtered through paper or mineral surfaces, with precise shadows and restrained green spill.
- **Dark mode:** deep infrastructure fields with local pale-neon activation, never ambient blue “future-tech” glow.

### Prohibited imagery shorthand

Reject generic robots, glowing brains, hologram hands, circuit globes, random nodes, ornamental particles, unearned floating devices, fake dashboards, and ungraded stock. AI is represented through observable retrieval, coordination, evidence, permission, uncertainty, failure, recovery, or state change.

## Anthropomorphism

The default range is levels `1–3`: implied behavior, object interaction, or a character-like form whose pose works without a face. Level `0` is essential for editorial and evidence-led rhythm. Levels `4–5` are exceptional. A permanent mascot is not approved.

Behavior comes before facial features. Every depiction must preserve a truthful capability boundary and avoid implied sentience, empathy, perfect knowledge, autonomous authority, or guaranteed outcomes. The complete decision system is documented in [SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN_ANTHROPOMORPHIC_SYSTEM.md](./SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN_ANTHROPOMORPHIC_SYSTEM.md).

## Iconography, controls, and micro-detail

Icons are functional evidence or control states only. They do not decorate a topic. A card, pill, badge, underline, arrow, plus sign, frame, or action rail requires a visible role supported by the adjacent message.

Micro-detail should answer at least one question:

- What happened?
- How does the system behave?
- What is the source or evidence?
- What creates the material atmosphere?
- What establishes scale or state?

Otherwise remove it. Fine detail must never destroy the first read or Arabic glyph clarity.

## Motion grammar

Motion expresses one operational verb and has anticipation, action, and a settled state. Appropriate verbs include organize, retrieve, connect, hand off, protect, resolve, recover, and scale. Decorative perpetual loops and meaningless particle drift are excluded.

Motion approval must preserve the static concept, capability boundary, typographic legibility, and final-state comprehension.

## Layout families, not templates

Families define reasoning and relationships; they never define fixed coordinates.

| Family | Best use | Required variation |
|---|---|---|
| Conceptual hero | Awareness, education, thought leadership | New metaphor, focal geometry, crop, and verbal–visual relation |
| Editorial statement | Brand, announcement, opinion | Authored type mass, field behavior, and image/type relationship |
| Product/system story | Product, service, case study | Real proof role, state, environment, and causal path |
| Data story | Statistic, evidence, transformation | Honest source, one thesis, visual encoding, and evidence hierarchy |
| Process explainer | Education, automation, workflow | Meaningful states and handoffs, not generic arrows or equal cards |
| Before/after | Transformation, case study | Specific tension, mechanism, and verified end state |
| Character narrative | Education, story, selected campaign work | Message-earned behavior and level; face-removal and ethics checks |
| Announcement | Launch, event, milestone | One event signal and distinctive typographic or material proposition |
| Arabic educational carousel | Arabic learning and thought leadership | One cognitive job per slide and Arabic-authored progression |

The observed corpus families and their biases are documented in [taxonomy.yaml](../principles/taxonomy.yaml). The rejected high-density promotional board is an internal review format, not a production family.

## Feed coherence

Each post receives one rhythm role: **anchor, breath, energy, information, or narrative**. Review at `3`, `9`, and `12` posts.

Reject a feed when:

- a focal silhouette or layout skeleton repeats without a sequence reason;
- the nearest nine posts use the same center of gravity or action rail;
- color intensity is monotonous;
- every post uses the same anthropomorphism level;
- information density lacks breath;
- the brand disappears when logos are hidden;
- individually strong posts create collective fatigue.

Recognition should come from recurring decisions—material response, light behavior, charged state, typographic voice, and operational storytelling—not repeated furniture.

## Reference and originality policy

The local corpus is copyrighted, read-only research material. It is a source of principles and quality comparisons, not production assets. The reference files, thumbnails, compositions, typography, characters, and graphics may not be copied, traced, redistributed, or sent to an image generator.

Each design brief must use at least three relevant references from at least two clusters and record:

- what principle was learned;
- why it applies;
- what expression must not be copied;
- how the SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN application is independently derived.

Generation receives the original SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN concept and extracted principles, with studio and campaign names removed. Professional reference pixels may be inspected locally by authorized critics for comparison; they are never a generation prompt. See [DESIGN_RETRIEVAL_SYSTEM.md](./DESIGN_RETRIEVAL_SYSTEM.md).

## Release proof

This visual language is a hypothesis until original SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN work proves it. A candidate does not pass because it follows this document. It must also:

1. execute a message-specific concept;
2. pass technical preflight;
3. survive original- and mobile-pixel critique by every required independent critic;
4. receive at least `145/160` from each required critic with no hard fail;
5. be judged comparable to relevant professional anchors;
6. pass originality and rights review;
7. avoid structural repetition in the `3/9/12`-post feed views;
8. decisively beat old-engine work on the identical brief;
9. receive separate, current-hash owner approval.

The scoring and evidence contract is defined in [CREATIVE_QUALITY_RUBRIC.md](./CREATIVE_QUALITY_RUBRIC.md). No current SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN output is designated gold; see [GOLDEN_SET.md](./GOLDEN_SET.md).

## Evidence boundaries

This system is grounded in a full inventory of `1,296` primary corpus covers, review of all `20` computational contact-sheet queues, selected original-pixel inspection, and external professional research. The corpus is overwhelmingly low-resolution landscape project-cover imagery, with no native square, portrait, `4:5`, or `9:16` examples. It can support broad concept, hierarchy, palette, and composition principles, but it cannot prove fine typography, micro-craft, Arabic shaping, or native social-format behavior. See [DESIGN_CORPUS_ANALYSIS.md](./DESIGN_CORPUS_ANALYSIS.md).

Accordingly, format-specific benchmarks and final-pixel review—not this document alone—decide whether the visual language works.
