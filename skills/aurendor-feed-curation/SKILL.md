# AURENDOR Feed Curation

## Exact job

Judge whether individually reviewed posts form a coherent, varied AURENDOR visual world. Coherence comes from shared grammar, material behavior, typography, and brand intelligence; variety comes from deliberate concept families, density, scale, palette mode, and rhythm.

## Required inputs

- Current rendered pixels and hashes for every candidate post.
- Approved purpose, visual family, imagery mode, feed role, language, and anthropomorphism level.
- Recent published/approved feed windows and prohibited repeated structures.
- Individual professional critique decisions; feed curation cannot rescue a failed asset.

## Review views

Build and inspect actual-pixel 3-, 9-, and 12-post contact views at realistic feed scale. Preserve post order and neutral gutters; do not recolor or crop posts to improve the board.

For each window, map:

- Rhythm role: `anchor`, `breath`, `energy`, `information`, or `narrative`.
- Light/dark balance and neon intensity.
- Concept family and imagery mode.
- Focal scale/position, center of gravity, negative-space shape, and density.
- Typography silhouette and Arabic/English distribution.
- Object/material/character recurrence and anthropomorphism level.
- Evidence/proof vs editorial vs promotional weight.

## Failure patterns

Reject or reorder a window with repeated hero position, identical card shells, the same headline block, a wall of deep green, constant neon, repeated split layouts, consecutive high-density posts, character overuse, or abrupt brand-world discontinuity. A logo and palette do not make a feed coherent.

## Decision

- **COHERENT:** recognizably one AURENDOR world with purposeful variation.
- **REORDER:** the set is strong but sequencing creates avoidable repetition or density problems.
- **REPLACE_CANDIDATE:** one individually acceptable post damages the system through repetition or wrong rhythm role.
- **BLOCKED:** missing current pixels/hashes or an individual asset lacks professional approval.

Return window-level evidence, repeated-structure fingerprints, proposed order, replacement constraints, and the next post’s target rhythm role. Do not prescribe a fixed template as the cure.

## Quality gate

Pass only when all inputs are current professionally reviewed pixels, each 3/9/12 window has a readable rhythm, Arabic presence is intentional, and repeated structures are justified rather than habitual.

## Failure behavior

If fewer posts exist than a window requires, assess the available window and label the larger view unavailable. If the only way to create coherence is to recolor or re-template strong posts, return to art direction for a better system rather than flattening the work.

## Eval cases

1. **Nine polished green cards:** Fail for template repetition.
2. **Different styles with shared grammar:** Can pass when typography/material/story rules connect them.
3. **Weak post improves color rhythm:** Still blocked; feed fit cannot override individual critique.
4. **Three dense posts in a row:** Reorder or replace with a breath role.
