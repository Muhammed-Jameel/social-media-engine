## What worked

- Arabic owns the hero moment; the object is subordinate to the message.
- The paper field, deep green, and controlled neon create a recognizably AURENDOR result without repeating the round-three machinery.
- `لا تبدأ بالأداة / ابدأ بالقرار` reads in under two seconds and remains clear at 324×405.
- Multiple states resolving into one route is a meaningful visual verb rather than a decorative AI symbol.

## What must change next

1. Replace the tangled incoming paths with exactly three deliberately spaced, non-crossing channels.
2. Reduce the internal neon glow so the decision block reads as frosted material, not an energy source.
3. Tighten the support copy to `وضوح القرار يجعل المسار قابلًا للأتمتة.` so it forms a calmer line block.
4. Preserve the headline position, paper field, single outgoing route, and canonical logo.
