# Iteration 01 — Decision Before Tool

## Objective

Create a new Arabic-first Instagram portrait post for Iraqi business and operations leaders. Communicate within two seconds that automation should begin with a defined decision, not with tool selection.

## Exact copy

- Metadata: `مبدأ تشغيلي · ١٣`
- Headline line 1: `لا تبدأ بالأداة`
- Headline line 2: `ابدأ بالقرار`
- Support: `حين يتّضح القرار، يصبح المسار قابلًا للأتمتة.`

## Concept tournament

1. **Selected — Decision lens:** a precise translucent green optical block turns several uncertain material paths into one accountable route. Strong textless state change; distinct from round-three machines and archives.
2. Rejected — giant Arabic type only: clear, but too close to existing editorial candidates `04` and `06`.
3. Rejected — contemporary office architecture: contextual, but the visual verb becomes atmosphere rather than decision clarity.
4. Rejected — anthropomorphic relay: behavior is relevant, but `03` already occupies that family and currently has an unresolved typography hard fail.

## Design system

- Canvas: 1080×1350; mobile review 324×405.
- Safe margin: 72 px; 12-column grid; copy occupies no more than five columns.
- Background: warm paper `#F4F8F5`, with deep green `#003F35` and neon `#0EDB23` used semantically.
- Typography: Ghroob Arabic ITF Bold; RTL; headline leading at least `1.04`; no decorative diacritics added; manual glyph and line-box inspection required.
- Main visual: original textless editorial still life, materially plausible, with one focal optical block and one converged path.
- Logo: canonical deep-green horizontal mark, bottom-left, quiet signature.
- Avoid: dark machine pedestal, generic robot/AI icon, dashboard UI, random glow, repeated round-three archive/relay geometry, extra text, copied reference composition.

## Quality target

Every applicable professional critic must independently reach 145/160 with no hard fail. Arabic typography and exact-copy review are mandatory; the current feed must gain a new light-field, right-weighted focal silhouette.
