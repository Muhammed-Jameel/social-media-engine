## Concept tournament

## Composition constraints

- Native HTML/CSS only with project Ghroob Arabic.

- No letter distortion, calligraphic imitation, rotation, arrow, number-as-hero, grid, object, image, UI, or external asset.
- Closing line remains short and at least 15 px in the explicit mobile reduction.

All critics ≥145/160; Arabic dots/diacritics, reading order, rhythm, contrast, mobile, originality, and feed gates required; production disabled.
