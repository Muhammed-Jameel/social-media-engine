# Iteration 19 — Pixel Review

## Verdict

**PASS — high-rhythm Arabic action sequence.**

Art direction 155, graphic design 156, social strategy 154, Arabic 155. Integrated score: **156/160**. No hard fail; benchmark-only.

## Why it passes

- Three intact Arabic verbs form a top-to-bottom diagonal from upper right toward lower left, creating motion that respects RTL spatial instinct.
- Scale and placement make the words both information and image without distorting letterforms or imitating custom calligraphy.
- The sequence is operational and memorable: ask, test, measure, then expand what works.
- The pale field, dark/accent alternation, sparse close, and small line create pacing distinct from the earlier dark giant-word poster.
- `اسأل`, `اختبر`, and the kasra in `قِس` remain immediately legible at 324×405.
- The closing line reaches approximately 15.6 px after the explicit mobile reduction, improving on earlier 12–13 px support sizes.

## Hard-fail audit

- Arabic exact copy, hamza, dots, kasra, shadda, punctuation, word integrity, and top-to-bottom order: pass.
- No letter rotation, distortion, outline imitation, pseudo-calligraphy, arrow, route, number-as-hero, object, image, interface, external asset, or reference pixel: pass.
- Contrast, logo, safe area, original/mobile dimensions, copy bounds, and hashes: pass.
- Production publication: disabled.

## Residual risk

Large verbs can become a reusable visual crutch if repeated. This candidate works because the three-step behavior is the content; future sequences need a different reading mechanism rather than substituting new large words into the same diagonal.

Do not reuse this three-verb diagonal, relative word positions, scale progression, pale field, or closing-line treatment as a template.
