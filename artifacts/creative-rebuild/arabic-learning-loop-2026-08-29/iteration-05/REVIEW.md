## What worked

- One real gesture performs the concept; the image does not need a dashboard, icon, or character.
- The hand is anatomically natural and the neutral sleeve suggests regional professional context without costume cliché.
- The branch and blocking tile remain visually legible after downsampling.
- Arabic owns the quiet upper field while the photograph supplies emotional and material credibility.
- The feed gains a human-scale photographic family distinct from both the optical still life and flat modular grid.

## Residual risk

Do not repeat the hand-on-path device. The next visual family should move away from paper routes, physical green strips, and before/after process diagrams.
