# Iteration 05 — Design for the Exception

## Objective

Teach a mature automation principle: a robust automated path must know when to stop and request a human decision.

## Exact copy

- Metadata: `مبدأ تشغيلي · ١٧`
- Headline line 1: `صمّم للاستثناء`
- Headline line 2: `لا للحالة المثالية`
- Support: `المسار الذكي يعرف متى يتوقّف ويطلب قرارًا.`

## Concept tournament

1. **Selected — human stop tile:** a documentary overhead crop of a real hand deliberately placing one translucent green decision tile at a branching paper path. Human gesture performs the exception-handling concept.
2. Rejected — red warning triangle: generic alarm symbolism, outside the brand palette, and too close to UI iconography.
3. Rejected — robot asking permission: clichéd AI imagery and likely to repeat the machine-heavy feed.
4. Rejected — portrait at a laptop: corporate-stock behavior with no visible visual verb.

## Design system

- 1080×1350 and 324×405.
- Exact Arabic overlaid in Ghroob, RTL, with generous line spacing.
- Clean upper-right copy field; human gesture and branch occupy the lower two-thirds.
- Warm paper/mineral material, deep green route, translucent pale/neon decision tile.
- Canonical AURENDOR logo only; no generated letters, logos, UI, or dashboards.
