## Why it passes

- The citation marker is the concept and an actual reading device; it does not decorate an unrelated headline.
- The source line names three operational provenance fields—source, version, and update date—without pretending the post is a full governance standard.
- The asymmetric side margin, strong rule, large negative space, and compact folio cues create a publication voice distinct from the feed's images, diagrams, and carousel.
- Changing the superscript from a bare Arabic `١` to a small outlined marker fixed its cursor-like appearance at mobile scale.
- The Arabic is independently authored, concise, and aligned with NIST's documented emphasis on origin, lineage, and upstream sources.
- Headline, marker, footnote, and logo remain readable at 324×405.

## Residual risk

The deliberately open lower field gives the post editorial confidence but reduces visual density compared with conventional social templates. It is appropriate as a pacing post inside the varied feed, not as a default layout for every principle.

Do not reuse this side margin, circular superscript marker, long footnote rule, or folio composition as a template.
