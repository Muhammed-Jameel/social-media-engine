## Learning proven in pixels

3. A flat modular system can diversify the feed without becoming dashboard UI.
4. A human gesture communicates exception handling more effectively than a warning icon.
5. Arabic type can carry a full post when scale, crop, and negative space are controlled.
6. Carousel frames need independent value plus a diagnostic-to-action arc.
7. A metric graphic should define a relationship without inventing a value.
8. Iraqi cultural grounding can live in contemporary geometry and material, without flags or landmarks.
9. A purposeful portrait and lower text anchor add human credibility and break the repeated top-right poster skeleton.
