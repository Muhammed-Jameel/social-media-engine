## What worked

- The flat modular grammar is genuinely different from the generated-object family.
- Arabic remains the clear authority and survives the mobile review.
- The before/after states are visually distinct without relying on UI microcopy.
- The piece uses only native project assets and original geometry.

## Required correction

1. Reverse the arrow so it points from right to left.
2. Reduce the corridor from saturated neon to pale brand green so it guides rather than dominates.
3. Tighten the after-state modules to an exact repeated grid.
4. Preserve copy, light field, RTL labels, and native-asset-only construction.
