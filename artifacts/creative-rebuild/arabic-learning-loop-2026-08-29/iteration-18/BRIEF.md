# Iteration 18 — Name the Costly Decision

## Objective

Create a social-native diagnostic prompt that makes the viewer identify one recurring decision burden instead of passively reading another principle.

## Exact copy

- Metadata: `سؤال تشغيلي · ٣٠`
- Question line 1: `أي قرار يستهلك`
- Question line 2: `وقت فريقك كل أسبوع؟`
- Prompt: `سمِّه بكلمتين.`

## Concept tournament

1. **Selected — blank answer line:** a centered question and one open line make response the visual action.
2. Rejected — platform poll UI: fake controls date quickly and outsource the visual hierarchy to an interface.
3. Rejected — giant Arabic question mark: eye-catching but turns punctuation into decoration instead of participation.
4. Rejected — clock or calendar object: literal, familiar, and another still-life metaphor.

## Composition constraints

- Centered reading structure; no upper-right stack.
- Short prompt at a larger mobile-effective size.
- One answer line only; it must feel writable without imitating a form field.
- Native HTML/CSS only; no generated image, person, object, route, grid, card, interface, control, QR code, giant symbol, or external asset.

## Quality target

All critics ≥145/160; Arabic, participation clarity, hierarchy, contrast, mobile, originality, and feed gates required; production disabled.
