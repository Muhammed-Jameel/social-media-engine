## Why it passes

- The centered question changes the viewer's role from reader to respondent and uses one blank line as the only interaction cue.
- The prompt asks for a two-word answer, making the response bounded enough to complete mentally or in a comment.
- The second render removed a glow that made the answer area resemble a form field; the final pixels contain one clean writable line only.
- The question uses an interrogative hook rather than another negated principle and keeps the support prompt unusually short and large.
- Centered type, centered logo, dark radial field, and open lower space add a new campaign silhouette without a generated image.
- All copy and the answer line remain immediately readable at 324×405.
