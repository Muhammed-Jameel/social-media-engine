# Iteration 15 — Pixel Review

## Verdict

**PASS — cinematic slow-transformation candidate.**

Art direction 156, graphic design 155, social strategy 153, Arabic 154. Integrated score: **155/160**. No hard fail; benchmark-only.

## Why it passes

- The same membrane visibly changes from compressed pleats to a relaxed flowing form, so motion carries the strategic idea rather than merely adding energy.
- The visual freezes a slow transition with controlled sharpness and blur; it avoids arrows, speed streaks, explosions, particles, and circular transformation clichés.
- Central dark negative space lets Arabic remain the highest-information layer while the material creates depth around it.
- `التغيير لا يبدأ بإعلان بل بعادة تتكرّر` is a concise, independently authored Arabic statement; the support turns it into one practical behavior.
- White, pale green, and support green all sit on the audited deep-green field with strong contrast.
- Headline, support, pleat-to-flow transition, and logo remain clear at 324×405.

## Hard-fail audit

- Arabic exact copy, hamza, tanween, shadda, punctuation, shaping, line order, and spacing: pass.
- Single continuous material and visible state change: pass.
- No person, product, seal, paper still life, route, grid, UI, arrow, pseudo-text, external asset, or reference pixel: pass.
- Palette, logo, safe area, original/mobile dimensions, copy bounds, and hashes: pass.
- Production publication: disabled.

## Residual risk

Without the Arabic, translucent fabric can suggest fashion, luxury, or atmosphere as readily as organizational change. The state transition is strong enough for this specific message, but the membrane cannot become a generic AURENDOR motif.

Do not reuse this pleat-to-flow geometry, dark central void, membrane material, or cinematic lighting rule as a template.
