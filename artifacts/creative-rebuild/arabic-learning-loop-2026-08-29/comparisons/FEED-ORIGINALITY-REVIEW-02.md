## Verdict

**PASS — coherent system with materially varied campaign families.**

The accepted feed now spans nine distinct visual behaviors:

1. optical object metaphor;
2. native process diagram;
3. photographed human intervention;
4. Arabic typographic poster;
5. three-frame diagnostic carousel;
6. single-metric information design;
7. physical Iraqi-modernist collage;
8. documentary human portrait;
9. cut-paper human illustration.

The invariants are the AURENDOR green system, Ghroob Arabic hierarchy, disciplined logo signature, operational subject matter, and restrained number of ideas per frame. The variables are medium, focal geometry, copy location, tonal field, density, human presence, and narrative mode.

## Originality audit

- Every accepted output has a unique pixel SHA-256; no external reference image was supplied to generation.
- The generated image families use different subject structures: optical block, hand-and-branch, architectural collage, single active portrait, and two-person clarification scene.
- Native layouts were built from project type, logo, and original HTML/CSS geometry rather than downloaded templates or icons.
- Public research informed abstract principles only; no source logo, copy, typeface, illustration, layout, or distinctive campaign pixel appears in the outputs.
- The carousel's three frames intentionally share a sequence system; they are one campaign family, not three claims of unrelated novelty.

## Remaining feed risks

- Several early accepted posts place metadata and headline at the upper right; Iterations 12 and 13 materially reduce this rhythm but do not erase it.
- Deep green plus pale paper dominates by brand necessity. Future variation should come from spatial behavior, material, light, crop, and content—not arbitrary off-brand color.
- The logo often occupies a lower corner at similar scale. A future system test may explore a controlled edge signature or vertical lockup only if the canonical asset rules permit it.
- Human work is now present in photography and illustration; the next post should not use another person-at-work scene.

## Next constraint

The next candidate must avoid people, split-screen fields, upright blank pages, top-right headline stacks, process routes, grids, and oversized isolated Arabic words. It should introduce a materially different editorial behavior while preserving exact Arabic and the brand system.
