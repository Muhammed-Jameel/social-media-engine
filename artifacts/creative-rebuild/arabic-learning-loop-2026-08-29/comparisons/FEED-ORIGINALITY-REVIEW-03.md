## System coherence

- Ghroob Arabic, the approved green/paper palette, the canonical logo, operational subject matter, and one-idea hierarchy make the campaign recognizable.
- Medium, focal silhouette, density, lighting, copy location, field tone, human presence, and explanatory behavior vary enough that the invariants do not become a template.
- The three carousel frames intentionally share one family system; counting them as a sequence is not an originality defect.
- The two rejected posts remain visible as learning evidence and are not included in the accepted feed.

## Originality evidence

- All accepted original files have different pixel SHA-256 values.
- Generated source images used text-only prompts and no external reference pixels.
- Public research contributed abstract principles only; no source project copy, identity, layout, person, motif, texture, or asset was reused.
- Native candidates use project-owned type/logo and original HTML/CSS geometry; no downloaded template or icon is present.
- Each accepted review contains a motif-specific reuse prohibition so a successful image does not silently become a production template.

## Remaining risks

- Right-aligned upper headline stacks still appear frequently. Later passes should use centered, lower, marginal, circular, or sequential reading structures when the concept supports them.
- Brand color discipline necessarily keeps the palette narrow. Variation must continue through material, scale, crop, depth, motion, density, and editorial behavior—not random color additions.
- Several principles use a `لا` negation hook. Future copy should diversify rhetorical form through questions, imperatives, observations, or constructive positive claims.
- Supporting lines are generally readable at the explicit mobile reduction but often approach 12–13 px; future concepts should favor shorter support copy at a larger effective size.

## Next constraint

The next candidate must use a positive or interrogative Arabic hook rather than `لا`, avoid an upper-right two-line stack, keep support copy shorter, and introduce a new social-native reading behavior without repeating a person, folio, seal, membrane, route, grid, carousel, or macro paper still life.
