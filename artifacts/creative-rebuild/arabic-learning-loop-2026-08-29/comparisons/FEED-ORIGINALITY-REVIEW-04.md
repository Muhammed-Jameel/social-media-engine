# Feed and Originality Review 04

**Artifact:** `accepted-feed-3x6-02-20.png`  
**Included:** 18 accepted candidates — Iteration 02 and Iterations 04–20  
**Excluded:** Iterations 01 and 03; their failures remain archived

## Verdict

**PASS — expanded system with stronger rhetorical and spatial range.**

Since the 3×5 review, the feed added:

- a centered participatory question with one writable line;
- a positive three-verb Arabic sequence arranged in an RTL diagonal;
- a two-color material print in which registration is the concept.

These additions directly address the previous risks: they avoid `لا` hooks, use centered and diagonal structures, enlarge support copy, and introduce new reading behavior without changing the brand palette.

## Current risks

- Negative hooks remain visible in the historical feed even though recent posts diversify the rhetoric.
- Image-led posts and native typographic posts are now balanced, but future passes should not alternate mechanically between those two categories.
- The palette remains intentionally narrow; accessibility and brand recognition are strong, but spatial invention must continue carrying variety.
- Eighteen accepted posts are enough to expose motif reuse quickly. Every later concept must be checked against the full 3×6 contact sheet, not only its immediate predecessor.

## Next constraint

The next candidate must avoid a full-frame principle headline, participatory question, three-step sequence, organic aperture, archive object, person, and dark cinematic field. Explore a compact evidence-led or service-explanation format whose visual hierarchy can live in the feed without becoming a dashboard.
