# Iteration 06 — Intelligence Needs Context

## Objective

Create a type-led Arabic post that explains a practical AI principle: intelligence without the right operating context becomes guesswork.

## Exact copy

- Metadata: `مبدأ ذكاء · ١٨`
- Headline line 1: `الذكاء بلا سياق`
- Headline line 2: `مجرّد تخمين`
- Support: `زوّد النظام بما يحتاجه ليقرّر، لا بما يسهل جمعه.`
- Graphic word: `السياق`

## Concept tournament

1. **Selected — context as visual mass:** the word `السياق` becomes one cropped typographic field, visibly supplying the missing foundation under the headline.
2. Rejected — database cylinder: generic technology iconography and too close to UI language.
3. Rejected — photographic folders: another paper-object world would repeat the accepted feed.
4. Rejected — invented Arabic calligraphy: would risk legibility and imitation; the project Ghroob font stays exact.

## Research applied

- Use letterforms as mass while preserving reading.
- One bold color field creates energy; micro-elements stay disciplined.
- Let the word materially perform its meaning.
- Vary the system through scale and spatial tension, not superficial recoloring.

## Originality and asset record

Native HTML/CSS, project Ghroob fonts, and canonical AURENDOR logo only. No generated asset, downloaded icon, custom traced lettering, or reference pixels.

## Quality target

All critics ≥145/160, no hard fail, 1080×1350 and 324×405, production disabled.
