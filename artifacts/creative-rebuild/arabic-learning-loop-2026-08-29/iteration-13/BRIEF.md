# Iteration 13 — A Decision Must Be Understood

## Objective

Teach a communication principle: a decision cannot be executed reliably if the people responsible for it do not understand it.

## Exact copy

- Metadata: `مبدأ وضوح · ٢٥`
- Headline line 1: `إن لم يُفهَم القرار`
- Headline line 2: `فلن يُنفّذ`
- Support: `صُغْه بلغة واضحة، واختبره مع من سيعمل به.`

## Concept tournament

1. **Selected — cut-paper clarification:** two anonymous coworkers face one blank page; one points and the other responds, making mutual understanding the action.
2. Rejected — speech bubbles: generic icon language and likely to introduce generated pseudo-text.
3. Rejected — open doorway: familiar clarity metaphor but no people or execution behavior.
4. Rejected — dense-versus-clean type demo: too close to Iteration 06's typographic family.

## Accessibility constraints

- Arabic remains live HTML/CSS in Ghroob, not generated pixels.
- Right-aligned lines within a left-positioned copy block; RTL reading order preserved.
- Headline leading ≥1.08 and support leading ≥1.38.
- Palette pairs measured against WCAG thresholds.
- Mobile review explicitly checks `يُفهَم`, `يُنفّذ`, and `صُغْه` dots/diacritics.

## Originality and rights

Generate the textless illustration without references. No external illustration, character, icon, typeface, real identity, or campaign pixel is supplied.

## Quality target

All critics ≥145/160; accessibility, Arabic, anatomy/gesture, and mobile checks required; production disabled.
