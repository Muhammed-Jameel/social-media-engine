## Manual review notes

- Faces and hands were reviewed in the source contact sheets before compositing.
- Iraqi settings and people are present where the concept benefits from human context.
- Still-life posts use tactile paper, glass, metal, fabric, and workshop materials rather than vector substitutes.
- Image crops preserve the focal action and a credible copy zone.
- Arabic is rendered only with the project font in live HTML, then rasterized after font loading.
- Headline and decorative elements occupy separate zones; no letter dot or mark is touched or hidden.
