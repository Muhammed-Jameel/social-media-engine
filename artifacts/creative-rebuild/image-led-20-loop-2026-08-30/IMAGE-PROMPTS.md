# Final built-in image-generation prompt set

## Shared production specification

- Use case: `photorealistic-natural`, `product-mockup`, or `ads-marketing` according to subject.
- Asset type: image source for an AURENDOR Arabic social post.
- Style: photorealistic high-end editorial campaign photography; natural human and material detail; restrained film grain; credible Iraqi context when people or places appear.
- Composition: vertical 4:5, deliberate focal crop, usable upper or upper-right negative space for separately rendered Arabic copy.
- Palette: deep green shadows, pale neutral highlights, occasional physically plausible neon-green practical accent.
- Required: realistic faces, hands, fabric, paper, glass, metal, architecture, shadows, and regional context.
- Prohibited: text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, readable UI, unrelated trademarks, diagrams, graphic overlays, decorative circles, generic glowing technology, stock-photo smiles, or distorted anatomy.

11. Macro inspection of the precision edge of a machined dark-metal component with one luminous finish line.

14. Designer's hand flips a refined modular hinged prototype back to its original position.
15. A blank decision note is preserved beneath glass while a later blank result envelope rests above it.
16. Repeated blank packets pass through realistic hands; one lifted translucent layer reveals the repeating pattern.
