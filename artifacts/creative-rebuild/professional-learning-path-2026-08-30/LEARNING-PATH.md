# AURENDOR Extraordinary Social Design Learning Path

## Quality reset

All earlier acceptance labels were reset before the blind cohort regrade. A technically correct render is not automatically professional, and a professional render is not automatically extraordinary. Iteration 35 was subsequently graded as a rebuild candidate under the new rubric.

The loop now optimizes the retained feed before it optimizes volume.

## Real dataset

The learning corpus has two linked parts:

1. `professional-reference-dataset.jsonl` — real public project pages selected across Arabic typography, Arab cultural identity, campaign/social systems, editorial information design, conceptual typography, material photography, and public-interest communication. Each record stores observable design signals and an explicit rights boundary. Source pixels are never training inputs for generation and are never copied into AURENDOR output.
2. `aurendor-output-dataset.json` — every AURENDOR original/mobile render with its file hash, dimensions, legacy review score, publication state, and objective image features. All former passes are relabeled `provisional`; retained/rebuild/retire decisions must be earned under the new rubric.

Objective image features diagnose sameness and technical risk; they do not decide aesthetic quality. Human exact-pixel review remains authoritative.

## Curriculum

### Stage 1 — Calibrate the eye

- Study each external stratum separately.
- For every reference, identify the one decision that makes the concept necessary rather than merely attractive.
- Extract hierarchy, spatial tension, Arabic behavior, material behavior, image-to-copy relationship, pacing, and system consistency.
- Record what must not be copied: composition, crop, object, lettering, palette, texture, or project pixel.

Exit condition: at least six references per major stratum and a written distinction between principle and surface style.

### Stage 2 — Learn failure, not only excellence

- Revisit retained rejections and the weakest provisional posts.
- Label failures with `FAILURE-TAXONOMY.md`.
- Create counterexamples: what a competent-but-generic solution would do, and what a conceptually necessary solution must do differently.
- Treat inflated self-scoring as a failure mode.

Exit condition: every low-level post has a named failure mechanism rather than a vague “does not feel premium” judgment.

### Stage 3 — Blind cohort regrade

- Review desktop pixels without reading the old score.
- Review 324×405 mobile pixels separately.
- Review the feed at thumbnail scale before opening individual posts again.
- Score with `EXTRAORDINARY-RUBRIC.md` and cite visible evidence for every score above 8.
- Compare posts against each other, not only against their own brief.

Exit condition: every post is labeled `retain`, `rebuild`, or `retire`; no former acceptance survives by default.

### Stage 4 — Rebuild the bottom cohort

- Start with the lowest-scoring 20–25%, not the newest post.
- Run a minimum four-concept tournament per rebuild.
- Change the concept mechanism, not merely font size, color, or spacing.
- Preserve evidence and Arabic accuracy while replacing weak art direction.
- Keep before/after evidence and explain why the new mechanism is more necessary.

Exit condition: each rebuilt post clears every professional floor or is retired.

### Stage 5 — Feed-level art direction

- Audit rhythm across light/dark density, type/image balance, scale, texture, emotional cadence, subject family, and visual mechanism.
- Remove adjacent conceptual clusters and stylistic twins.
- Check that AURENDOR feels like one authored system without looking like one template.

Exit condition: no visible low-level cell, no repeated shortcut, and no sequence of three posts with the same evidence or composition behavior.

### Stage 6 — Extraordinary new work

- Resume new candidates only after the retained feed passes Stage 5.
- Select the professional-reference stratum before ideation, then deliberately avoid its surface features.
- Require one sentence explaining why the visual mechanism could not be swapped into an unrelated post.
- Reject any concept whose main idea is a card, giant number, generic icon, decorative AI image, or unearned metaphor.

## Review cadence

1. Evidence and Arabic boundary review.
2. Four-concept tournament.
3. Exact desktop render.
4. Mobile render.
5. Arabic/geometry junction audit under `ARABIC-GEOMETRY-PRODUCTION-RUBRIC.md` whenever shapes and Arabic share a frame.
6. Three independent critic passes: concept/art direction, graphic/Arabic craft, and social/feed performance.
7. Feed collision review.
8. Technical hash/dimension/publication audit.
9. Retain, rebuild, or retire. No “pass with reservations.”

## Completion definition

The retained feed has no post below 88/100, no core dimension below 8/10, no Arabic/mobile/evidence dimension below 9/10, and no hard fail. “Extraordinary” posts score at least 93/100 with no dimension below 9/10. The objective is not to make every post visually loud; it is to make every decision intentional, specific, defensible, and unmistakably authored.

Production and publishing remain disabled throughout learning and rebuild work.
