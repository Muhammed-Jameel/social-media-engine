# Extraordinary Quality Rubric — 100 Points

## Scored dimensions

Each dimension is scored 0–10. A score above 8 requires a visible, specific justification.

1. **Conceptual necessity** — the visual mechanism is inseparable from this exact idea; it is not a reusable decoration.
2. **Composition and hierarchy** — scale, tension, spacing, rhythm, crop, and eye path feel authored at full size and thumbnail size.
3. **Arabic typographic craft** — shaping, RTL logic, line breaks, punctuation, diacritics, proportions, and optical alignment are deliberate.
4. **Image/material craft** — photography, illustration, geometry, texture, light, and edge quality are convincing and coherent.
5. **Brand distinctiveness** — unmistakably AURENDOR without relying on palette and logo alone.
6. **Social stopping power** — the first read is immediate, the second read rewards attention, and the post earns its place in a feed.
7. **Evidence integrity** — source, population, period, uncertainty, inference, and metaphor boundaries are accurate in pixels and archive.
8. **Mobile resilience** — meaning, Arabic, source, image crop, and hierarchy survive at exactly 324×405.
9. **Originality and feed distance** — no collision in silhouette, mechanism, material, evidence behavior, or emotional cadence.
10. **Finish and detail** — no weak alignment, accidental tangent, muddy contrast, generic shadow, awkward crop, microtype, or unpolished edge.

## Tiers

- 93–100: extraordinary; no dimension below 9.
- 88–92: professional retained floor; no dimension below 8, and Arabic/mobile/evidence each at least 9.
- 80–87: competent but below AURENDOR retained level; rebuild.
- 70–79: weak/generic; rebuild from a new concept or retire.
- Below 70: retire.

## Hard fails

- Arabic error, broken RTL, malformed glyph, or misleading wording.
- Source, year, population, denominator, scope, or causal claim is wrong or hidden.
- Source line is not readable at mobile size.
- Generic template-card composition is the primary idea.
- Giant statistic substitutes for art direction.
- Literal icon or cliché carries the concept without transformation.
- Professional reference pixels, layout, object arrangement, or distinctive treatment are copied.
- Generated image contains invented evidence, text, brand, interface, or unreviewed cultural specificity.
- Visible collision with another retained AURENDOR post.
- Publication is enabled during the benchmark loop.

## Required reviewer evidence

Every retained score sheet must include:

- one sentence proving conceptual necessity;
- three visible craft observations;
- mobile type-size and crop observations;
- one closest-feed-neighbor comparison;
- evidence/inference boundary;
- one reason the post is more than technically clean;
- explicit absence of every applicable hard fail.

When Arabic text shares a frame with lines, circles, wedges, borders, masks, or perspective geometry, retention also requires 20/20 under `ARABIC-GEOMETRY-PRODUCTION-RUBRIC.md`. Any accidental contact with a dot, diacritic, ligature, or terminal glyph is an automatic fail.
