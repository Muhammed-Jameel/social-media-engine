# Arabic + Geometry Learning Loop 01

## Trigger

User review identified the remaining production weakness: line/circle construction and imperfect optical alignment between Arabic words and geometric shapes, especially where letter dots touched or disappeared behind geometry.

## Target cohort

- Iteration 39 — readiness loop/axis.
- Iteration 40 — three convergence wedges.
- Iteration 41 — signal-to-decision corridor.

## Changes

### Iteration 39

- Removed rotated Arabic from the diagonal axis.
- Removed circular Arabic microtype whose dots competed with the dashed ring.
- Replaced it with mathematically uniform dashes.
- Reduced ring stroke from 84 px to 64 px and dash stroke from 12 px to 10 px.
- Created separate upper and lower quiet zones inside the ring.
- Reduced and repositioned the lower question until the full word cleared both axis and ring.

Result: Arabic clearance 5/5, shape precision 5/5, optical alignment 5/5, mobile survival 5/5 — **20/20**.

### Iteration 40

- Narrowed each wedge label zone from 300 px to 200 px at the stable wide end.
- Reduced label type from 44 px to 38 px.
- Recentered each label vertically inside the local wedge height rather than the overall SVG bounds.
- Increased vertical separation between the condition, action headline, and operating recipe.

Result: Arabic clearance 5/5, shape precision 5/5, optical alignment 5/5, mobile survival 5/5 — **20/20**.

### Iteration 41

- Increased vertical clearance between `إشارة` and the signal circle.
- Moved `قرار` completely outside the perspective field, with a separate dark-canvas quiet zone below the decision boundary.
- Expanded the central label knockout and its padding around `زمن القرار`.
- Preserved integer coordinates and consistent perspective-frame stroke progression.

Result: Arabic clearance 5/5, shape precision 5/5, optical alignment 5/5, mobile survival 5/5 — **20/20**.

## New operating rule

Every remaining rebuild now receives the 20-point Arabic/geometry supplement after the normal 100-point rubric. Any accidental dot/shape contact returns the candidate to rebuild regardless of its main score.

Production and publishing remain disabled.
