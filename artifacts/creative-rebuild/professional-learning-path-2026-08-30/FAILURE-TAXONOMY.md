# AURENDOR Design Failure Taxonomy

## Concept failures

- `CF01_TEMPLATE_CARD` — boxes and panels organize content but do not embody the idea.
- `CF02_GIANT_NUMBER` — an oversized statistic substitutes for a concept.
- `CF03_LITERAL_ICON` — a generic device, bolt, brain, robot, chart, or symbol carries the whole message.
- `CF04_SWAPABLE_METAPHOR` — the visual could be reused for several unrelated posts.
- `CF05_DECORATIVE_AI` — generated atmosphere is attractive but semantically unearned.
- `CF06_UNEARNED_DRAMA` — visual intensity exceeds the evidence or subject.

## Craft failures

- `CR01_WEAK_HIERARCHY` — no decisive first, second, and third read.
- `CR02_DEAD_SPACE` — emptiness is accidental rather than tension or breathing room.
- `CR03_MICRO_SOURCE` — source or support becomes functionally unreadable at mobile size.
- `CR04_GENERIC_SHADOW` — stock-card depth or effects reduce credibility.
- `CR05_ARABIC_OPTICAL` — technically shaped Arabic is optically awkward, mechanically centered, or poorly broken.
- `CR06_UNRESOLVED_EDGE` — crop, tangent, clipping, or alignment looks accidental.
- `CR07_MATERIAL_MISMATCH` — image, type, texture, and geometry belong to different visual worlds.

## Strategy failures

- `ST01_CAPTION_DEPENDENCE` — pixels do not contain enough evidence or context.
- `ST02_LOW_STOPPING_POWER` — correct information looks like an internal slide rather than social creative.
- `ST03_ONE_READ_ONLY` — immediate headline exists but there is no second layer of reward.
- `ST04_FEED_TWIN` — silhouette, mechanism, material, or evidence behavior repeats a retained neighbor.
- `ST05_CLUSTER_FATIGUE` — too many adjacent posts share topic or emotional cadence.
- `ST06_BRAND_WALLPAPER` — palette and logo are doing more identity work than art direction.

## Evidence failures

- `EV01_SCOPE_DRIFT` — city, segment, year, population, or denominator becomes broader in the post.
- `EV02_CAUSAL_LEAP` — observed association or condition becomes a cause or promised outcome.
- `EV03_METAPHOR_AS_EVIDENCE` — image is allowed to imply facts the source does not contain.
- `EV04_PRECISION_THEATER` — exact numbers are shown without needed context or with false currentness.

## Process failures

- `PR01_SCORE_INFLATION` — cleanliness or constraint compliance is scored as exceptional craft.
- `PR02_SINGLE_CONCEPT` — first workable idea is rendered without a meaningful tournament.
- `PR03_POLISH_ONLY_REBUILD` — weak concept receives spacing/color tweaks instead of replacement.
- `PR04_REFERENCE_SURFACE_COPY` — a principle study silently becomes a layout or style imitation.
