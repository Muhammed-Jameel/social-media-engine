## Pixel evidence and revision calls

### 01 — context handoff

- At mobile scale, the headline remains the first read and the crystal seam remains the second; the physical discontinuity explains the message before the support line.
- The upper-right Arabic mass counterbalances the lower-left ceramic plane without resorting to a card or generic tech field.
- The localized green line acts as state evidence, not decoration. The material edges are largely convincing, though the mineral insert is slightly pristine against the rough ceramic.
- Compared with `ref_51e708951ea61f18` and `ref_6dd0a9246092f4d5`, the idea is similarly singular and fast, with marginally less retouch micro-detail visible at the contact seam.
- **Next:** retain as benchmark candidate; inspect the mineral-to-ceramic contact at 200% and run originality review. No concept restart.

### 02 — RAG evidence archive

- The archive dominates at mobile size while one filament and one pale fragment make retrieval and provenance discoverable on a second beat.
- Dark tonal separation, material variety, and the returning line create depth without generic purple glow, floating UI, or a database icon.
- The grey support line is the weakest mobile element; it approaches the image boundary and should gain slightly more contrast/clearance before release.
- Compared with `ref_4afce40fe49062b6`, `ref_a549cc94cfd3fc44`, and `ref_79573233e0b609f3`, this has comparable world-building and a more singular story, but less small-scale product proof.
- **Next:** raise support contrast and separate it from the archive silhouette by roughly 20–28 px; keep concept.

### 03 — automation relay

- The Arabic promise reads immediately, and the three objects create a coherent material family; the warm negative field works well in the feed.
- Without copy, the left object can read as a lens and the other two as industrial apertures. The responsibility transfer and narrow capability boundary are not yet visibly enacted.
- The green charge remains inside the left module instead of being perceptibly handed through the middle into the receiving slot.
- This falls below the behavioral storytelling of `ref_2ca843f06643d5f6` and the material-system consistency of `ref_79573233e0b609f3`.
- **Next:** regenerate the asset so the same token is physically present between modules, with sequential apertures visibly aligned. If that execution still fails, return to art direction.

### 04 — decision question

- **Next:** bind the lower charge directly to the question-mark/decision mechanism or remove it and use one clearer visual consequence. Keep the headline route; revise execution.

## Feed and old-versus-new verdict

The identical-brief comparison is a decisive win for the new candidate. The old design distributes attention across border, logo, label, headline, process cards, explanatory paragraph, and action rail; the new design gives the audience one claim, one physical consequence, and one quiet support beat. The improvement is observable at mobile scale without using metadata.

Across 12 posts, the feed is coherent through material deep green, paper, one localized green charge, Ghroob/Ranclo, and a quiet logo signature. It is not one repeated template: photo/material anchors, data split, before/after, aperture announcement, carousel mechanisms, and type-led breaths create real silhouette changes. The risk is that slides 09–11 and posts 04/05 lean on abstract central mechanisms; the next feed should restore a concrete case/product proof and a human/contextual photograph rather than multiplying diagram posts.
