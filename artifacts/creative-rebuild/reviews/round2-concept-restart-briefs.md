# Round 2 Concept-Restart Briefs

**Scope:** concept restart only for `03-ar-automation-relay`, `05-en-before-after`, `07-en-evidence-ledger`, and `12-en-complexity-answerable`  
**Role:** Senior Art Director  
**Status:** selected routes are production briefs, not approvals  
**Round-one evidence:** `senior-art-director-round1.json` and `senior-graphic-designer-round1.json`

## Rules shared by all four restarts

- Start from the communication problem, not the rejected composition. Do not reuse the prior still life, headline-plus-diagram stack, card-plus-flow stack, or slogan-only poster.
- Every selected route must communicate its operating verb with all copy, labels, logo, and color removed. Color may clarify state only after the silhouette and physical relationship work.
- Reference-corpus images remain critic-only. Do not send raw reference pixels, source paths, creator names, studio names, campaign names, or recognizably derived compositions to an image generator.
- Assets may be original native vectors, original 3D scenes, AURENDOR-owned evidence, or rights-cleared original photography. Generated imagery must be created from the original mechanism in this document and contain no type or logo; type is composed afterward from verified production files.
- One material story per route. Green charge means a selected, verified, transferred, or stopped state. It is not ambient decoration.
- Design at `324 × 405` first, then resolve `1080 × 1350`. A mechanism that needs a caption or zoom to become intelligible fails before craft review.
- No invented customer result, fake product UI, or unsupported autonomy claim. Any illustrative artifact must say so at a readable support tier in the final composition.
- The selected route is provisional. If the textless test fails, return to the other routes; do not rescue it with arrows, icons, labels, or additional copy.

## 03 — Reliable automation is a controlled relay

### What round one failed to show

The three industrial forms were materially coherent but behaved as a passive product still life. No payload moved, no responsibility changed hands, no verification occurred, and no human stop altered the sequence. With copy hidden, the scene read as lenses and apertures rather than reliable automation.

### Fixed communication contract

- **Audience tension:** automation is often sold as invisible magic, while operators need bounded steps, observable transfer, and the power to stop.
- **Two-second takeaway:** one responsibility is deliberately passed, checked, received, and remains interruptible.
- **Direction of action:** right-to-left, so the operating action and Arabic entry agree without mechanically mirroring an English scene.
- **Capability truth:** the system executes bounded steps; it does not think, improvise, or possess autonomous authority.
- **Anthropomorphism ceiling:** level `2`; behavior and posture only, no eyes, mouth, hands, mascot anatomy, or implied sentience.

### Route 03-A — Custody Baton — **SELECTED**

- **Visual family / material:** object-interaction narrative; machined dark ceramic, brushed metal contacts, one clear mineral-glass baton with a narrow green core.
- **Textless mechanism:** a single baton is visibly mid-transfer from a released cradle on the right into a verifying clamp at center. The receiving bay on the left is aligned and waiting. Beyond it, a large manual stop blade physically intersects the rail. The right cradle is open and empty, the center clamp visibly holds the same baton, and the left bay is not yet charged. The three roles are distinct through state and contact, not labels.
- **Static story:** anticipation is the open receiving bay; action is the baton held between release and verification contacts; consequence is the clearly available stop blade before the final bay. One frame shows ordered custody without duplicating the baton.
- **Asset plan:** build an original 3D scene or controlled 3D/vector hybrid. Model the baton, three role-specific contacts, rail, and manual stop as separate geometry. Render without type or logo. Composite only exact vector state marks afterward. Do not ask image generation to invent machinery, duplicated tokens, text, or precision contacts.
- **Crop / depth:** a shallow three-quarter view runs from upper right to lower left through the middle `58–64%` of the portrait. Crop the source cradle partly at the right edge and the manual stop partly at the left edge so the system continues beyond the frame. Keep the action plane large enough that the baton is at least `17%` of canvas width. The lower `24–28%` is a calm typographic plinth, not an empty tabletop.
- **Typography relationship:** place the Arabic thesis on the lower-right plinth, aligned to the baton axis. The first line names reliability; the second line completes the visible transfer rather than describing the hardware. One short support line may sit under the rail only if it remains readable at mobile size. Do not put microcopy across object contacts. Green may emphasize only the Arabic word associated with transfer or responsibility—not both and not as an underline.
- **Why this closes the gaps:** responsibility is one persistent object; transfer is an observable contact change; verification has a unique physical role; the human stop is a real obstruction rather than a footer claim. The route retains the useful industrial world while replacing the rejected passive poses.
- **Primary risk:** three stations can still read as a conveyor. Counter this by making each contact geometry and state unmistakably different and by showing the baton physically bridging release and verification.

### Route 03-B — Indexed Seal

- **Visual family / material:** macro papercraft and precision tooling; dense paper/mineral process ticket, embossed ceramic dies, one metal stop key.
- **Textless mechanism:** a single thick ticket passes right-to-left through three close-set stations. Each station leaves a visible, cumulative physical notch or seal on the same ticket. The final gate is held open by a removable human key; pulling it blocks the ticket.
- **Asset plan:** original top-lit miniature or 3D macro scene. Create the ticket and embossed marks natively; no pseudo-text. Capture the ticket continuously through all stations so it cannot appear as three unrelated cards.
- **Crop:** near-orthographic top-down band across the lower two-thirds; the Arabic headline occupies a protected upper-right field and one station intentionally breaks the grid at center.
- **Typography relationship:** the headline baseline follows the ticket's direction but never sits on the ticket. A small, readable `illustrative behavior` qualifier is permitted because no literal product mechanism is being claimed.
- **Why it could work:** cumulative marks make ordered responsibility and verification visible. It is calmer and more editorial than route A.
- **Why it is not selected:** it risks reading as document processing or manufacturing rather than an accountable relay, and the human stop is less immediate.

### Route 03-C — Counterbalanced Handoff

- **Visual family / material:** kinetic sculpture; three weighted arms, one ceramic capsule, exposed pivots and a mechanical brake.
- **Textless mechanism:** the right arm has just released the capsule into the center arm; the left arm cannot rise until the center arm settles. A human-scale brake lever visibly holds the final pivot.
- **Asset plan:** precision 3D or practical miniature only; the balance, contacts, gravity, and shadows must be physically plausible. No motion trails, duplicated capsules, or floating parts.
- **Crop:** diagonal low-angle crop with the capsule at the exact center of gravity; the Arabic type sits in the negative wedge created by the rising left arm.
- **Typography relationship:** two compact Arabic lines counterweight the sculpture rather than floating above it. Type remains secondary to the capsule transfer.
- **Why it could work:** interdependence and ordered timing become physical rather than diagrammatic.
- **Why it is not selected:** mechanical plausibility and single-frame readability are high-risk; a failed balance would look like an art object rather than automation.

### Route 03-D — Permission Shutters

- **Visual family / material:** level-1 architectural cutaway; three deep chambers, a physically constrained light field, hard-edged shutters, one manual override.
- **Textless mechanism:** a pale-green signal passes only through open shutters. One chamber is active, one is verifying through a narrow aperture, and one remains dark behind a closed human-controlled shutter.
- **Asset plan:** native 3D or vector cutaway with exact occlusion and light boundaries. Light must terminate on physical surfaces and cannot become a magical glow.
- **Crop:** vertical architectural section occupies the left `65%`; Arabic type enters from the right and aligns with the one open aperture.
- **Typography relationship:** the copy completes the ethics of permission and stop; it does not label the chambers.
- **Why it could work:** bounded capability and human authority are extremely clear without character forms.
- **Why it is not selected:** the transfer of responsibility is weaker than the baton route and could collapse into generic security/infrastructure imagery.

### Selected-route acceptance test

At mobile size with all copy and logo hidden, an unprimed reviewer must be able to say: “one object is being passed through different roles, it is currently being checked, and the process can be physically stopped.” If reviewers say camera, robot parts, product lineup, or conveyor, route 03-A fails and must not advance.

## 05 — From chasing status to seeing the next action

### What round one failed to show

The dotted wandering line and straight line were a generic process-diagram trope. The headline dominated while the evidence was tiny, the middle of the canvas was inert, and the disclaimer became footer texture. The visual did not make repeated status chasing or ownership change concrete.

### Fixed communication contract

- **Audience tension:** teams waste attention asking for updates because no one accountable record exposes what happens next.
- **Two-second takeaway:** repeated follow-up pressure becomes one owned, visible next action.
- **Truth condition:** this remains an illustrative operating pattern, not a client result or quantified improvement.
- **Anthropomorphism:** level `0–1`; no characters are needed.

### Route 05-A — Accountability Press — **SELECTED**

- **Visual family / material:** conceptual transformation; paper-fiber status loops, one dark mineral task block, a machined ownership die, one pale output tab with a green leading edge.
- **Textless mechanism:** several repeated paper loops enter from the left, circle back, and crowd around a sealed task block. At the center, one ownership die physically locks into a uniquely shaped socket. That action compresses the loose loops into a single thick record, and one arrow-shaped next-action tab exits decisively to the right. The input is repetition without progress; the output is one owned direction.
- **Static story:** the left loops are visibly still loose; the die is caught at the instant of locking; the right tab has begun to emerge. One frame contains before, action, and after without a conventional split screen.
- **Asset plan:** build an original practical-paper miniature, precise 3D scene, or 3D/native-vector hybrid. Use real paper edge behavior, a manufactured socket, and one continuous output record. Do not use app cards, notification icons, chat bubbles, dotted process lines, or fabricated UI. Any word on the output tab is added natively after rendering; the textless silhouette must already read as an actionable exit.
- **Crop / depth:** top-down or `20–30°` oblique macro. Let incoming loops crop off the left and lower edges; place the ownership die slightly right of center; let the output tab break the right edge. The transformation occupies the middle `55%` of the canvas, eliminating the dead central field. Preserve one calm upper band for the thesis and one readable lower band for qualification.
- **Typography relationship:** set `FROM CHASING STATUS` as a smaller, high-legibility entry phrase whose left edge follows the returning loops. Set `SEE THE NEXT ACTION` larger and aligned with the outgoing tab. Dh Ranclo may accent only `NEXT`; all other English and the qualification use the approved reading face. The readable line `Illustrative operating pattern — not a client result` sits directly beneath the output, not at the footer.
- **Why this closes the gaps:** repeated chasing is a visible looping material, ownership is a physical commitment rather than a label, and next action has dominant scale. The proof occupies the focal plane, so the composition cannot be reused for unrelated copy without changing the mechanism.
- **Primary risk:** it can become generic chaos-to-order. The uniquely keyed ownership die and visible return loops are mandatory; simple tangled strands becoming straight are insufficient.

### Route 05-B — Status Echo Chamber

- **Visual family / material:** architectural object story; one sealed task at the center of several repeated mail slots, with a single owned exit door.
- **Textless mechanism:** identical request slips enter from multiple perimeter slots and visibly accumulate around the same opaque task. One keyed owner door opens and releases a single forward-facing action ticket; the other slots close.
- **Asset plan:** original architectural 3D cutaway with one continuous pile of repeated slips. No envelope icons or interface chrome.
- **Crop:** the circular chamber fills the canvas off-center; the open owner door creates a strong right-side exit and type field.
- **Typography relationship:** the headline bends only once around the chamber's outer arc; the qualification sits beside the released ticket at evidence size.
- **Why it could work:** repetition and one accountable exit are visible, and the form is unlike the rejected infographic.
- **Why it is not selected:** a circular chamber risks looking like a mailbox, voting machine, or decorative portal, and its story needs more environmental explanation.

### Route 05-C — Four Shadows, One Commitment

- **Visual family / material:** original studio photography or physically accurate 3D; one task object, four conflicting lights, one locking prism.
- **Textless mechanism:** four moving light sources cast four contradictory shadows of the same task, making status uncertain. A single ownership prism locks into place and aligns the shadows into one crisp arrow pointing to the next action.
- **Asset plan:** controlled studio setup or ray-traced 3D with exact light direction. No atmospheric glow; every shadow must have a source and the arrow must be physically produced.
- **Crop:** one low task block fills the lower center; shadows extend upward into the type field and resolve at a single rightward direction.
- **Typography relationship:** type sits outside the shadow geometry; one phrase may be partially revealed only after alignment.
- **Why it could work:** it is memorable and makes ambiguity versus clarity physical.
- **Why it is not selected:** the ownership change is less explicit, and the shadow engineering is likely to become clever but difficult at mobile size.

### Route 05-D — Auditable Case File

- **Visual family / material:** evidence-led editorial; a deliberately fictional, AURENDOR-owned operating record shown as a top-down case file.
- **Textless mechanism:** repeated duplicate status slips are visibly crossed and clipped behind one chronological decision record. A large owner tab and one exposed next-action field are physically attached to the front record.
- **Asset plan:** create a rights-safe illustrative record from scratch with no customer names, results, or fake product interface. Add all text natively in a high-legibility face and mark it illustrative.
- **Crop:** the record enters from the lower-right and is cropped at large scale; discarded duplicates recede into the upper-left.
- **Typography relationship:** the headline uses the case-file margin and the next-action field becomes the second typographic beat.
- **Why it could work:** it supplies real information hierarchy and honest evidence rather than decorative diagrams.
- **Why it is not selected:** the idea depends more heavily on reading fields and is weaker in the mandated textless test.

### Selected-route acceptance test

With words removed, reviewers must perceive repeated return or checking on the left, one act of ownership at center, and a single forward action on the right. If the scene reads only as a paper shredder, factory, or generic untangling, route 05-A fails.

## 07 — Every important decision keeps its evidence

### What round one failed to show

The wide card said “evidence,” but no evidence artifact appeared. A labelled path substituted for proof, operational labels failed at mobile size, and the headline-card-flow architecture looked ready to accept any B2B product message.

### Fixed communication contract

- **Audience tension:** decisions lose trust when the source, owner, and rationale detach from the outcome.
- **Two-second takeaway:** the decision record and the evidence that supports it remain physically inseparable.
- **Truth condition:** the benchmark may show an explicitly illustrative owned record, not a customer record and not a claim that a shipping product already performs this behavior.
- **Anthropomorphism:** level `0–1`; attachment and custody carry the story.

### Route 07-A — Provenance Spine — **SELECTED**

- **Visual family / material:** evidence-led editorial object; one oversized folio built from paper/mineral pages, a transparent evidence sleeve, an opaque decision plate, and a continuous mineral-glass spine with one green internal filament.
- **Textless mechanism:** the folio is open in a three-quarter view. The opaque decision plate lifts from the page, but it cannot separate because the same visible spine pierces both the decision plate and the transparent sleeve containing a source fragment. An owner notch and next-action tab are cut into the same bound edge. Moving the decision necessarily moves its evidence.
- **Evidence artifact:** create one AURENDOR-owned illustrative decision record from a neutral fictional operating scenario. It contains a source excerpt, source identifier, decision, owner, timestamp, and next action. No customer data, fabricated metric, or pseudo-interface. The visible artifact must be real, legible, and explicitly marked `ILLUSTRATIVE RECORD` at evidence size.
- **Asset plan:** build the record and all typography natively. Create the folio, sleeve, spine, contacts, and shadows in original 3D or a practical miniature. Render the object without embedded generated text. Composite the owned record into the transparent sleeve afterward with correct perspective, refraction, occlusion, and focus.
- **Crop / depth:** the bound spine runs from lower center toward upper right and carries the dominant diagonal. The folio fills `62–70%` of the canvas and crops off the lower and right edges. The transparent evidence sleeve is foreground; the lifted decision plate is the action plane; the source context remains visible beneath. Reserve a narrow upper-left counterfield for the headline rather than rebuilding a horizontal card stack.
- **Typography relationship:** `THE DECISION` sits close to the opaque plate; `KEEPS ITS EVIDENCE` aligns with the uninterrupted spine and may use green only on `EVIDENCE`. Use Dh Ranclo for one short display signal at most; the record, source, owner, timestamp, next action, and illustrative qualification require the approved high-legibility companion. The headline must name the consequence, not label the folio.
- **Why this closes the gaps:** an actual owned source record is visible; physical binding—not labelled nodes—proves provenance; the selected crop gives one object dominant agency; required operational text is readable because it belongs to the evidence artifact rather than decorative microcopy.
- **Primary risk:** it can look like premium stationery. The transparent sleeve must expose a credible source fragment, and the spine must visibly bind source and decision under movement.

### Route 07-B — Tamper-Evident Decision Capsule

- **Visual family / material:** bespoke 3D containment; clear glass capsule, opaque decision core, source wafer, one unbroken verification seal.
- **Textless mechanism:** the decision core and source wafer occupy separate layers inside one sealed capsule. A continuous green tamper line crosses both; opening one layer visibly breaks the whole seal.
- **Asset plan:** precision 3D with physically plausible glass, gasket, seal, refraction, and contact. Record content is composited natively and must remain legible through the glass.
- **Crop:** extreme macro of the seal crossing the decision and source layers; the capsule continues beyond top and bottom edges.
- **Typography relationship:** the headline follows the seal axis in adjacent negative space; no type is printed on generated surfaces.
- **Why it could work:** chain of custody and inseparability are immediately physical.
- **Why it is not selected:** a capsule can imply security or immutability guarantees beyond the intended product principle, and glass craft is high-risk.

### Route 07-C — Evidence Is Load-Bearing

- **Visual family / material:** conceptual photomanipulation or 3D sculpture; a heavy decision slab supported by one precise evidence wafer.
- **Textless mechanism:** the decision remains level only because a source wafer occupies a keyed structural joint. An adjacent empty joint causes the slab to tilt, demonstrating what happens when evidence is missing.
- **Asset plan:** original material sculpture with exact gravity, contact shadows, keyed joints, and no floating components. A small owned source record can be composited on the wafer.
- **Crop:** low close angle places the evidence joint at the focal center and lets the decision slab dominate the upper half.
- **Typography relationship:** the headline counterweights the tilted empty side; type never sits on the slab.
- **Why it could work:** evidence becomes consequential rather than administrative.
- **Why it is not selected:** it communicates that evidence supports a decision, but not that evidence travels with it or preserves custody.

### Route 07-D — Auditable Decision Strip

- **Visual family / material:** native evidence diagram with a physical strip rather than cards; one continuous vertical record bearing source, decision, owner, and next-action windows.
- **Textless mechanism:** the strip passes through a viewing aperture; every window is physically cut from the same substrate and connected by one uninterrupted edge code. Removing the source section breaks the strip and prevents the decision section from advancing.
- **Asset plan:** native vector and 3D paper-depth hybrid. Use a fully owned illustrative record and render all text natively.
- **Crop:** the strip runs vertically through the left two-thirds and breaks both top and bottom edges; the headline occupies the right counterfield.
- **Typography relationship:** the headline points to the unbroken record as one object, not to individual labelled stages.
- **Why it could work:** it is highly legible and easy to make auditable at mobile size.
- **Why it is not selected:** it remains closer to an operating diagram and therefore carries more template risk than the provenance folio.

### Selected-route acceptance test

With all words removed, reviewers must see one decision object and one distinct evidence artifact physically bound by the same spine, such that moving one necessarily moves the other. If the result reads as a notebook, generic folder, floating card, or luxury stationery arrangement, route 07-A fails.

## 12 — Make complexity answerable

### Why this route is being restarted despite a strong round-one score

The typographic poster had excellent hierarchy and brand recognition, but one independent craft review assigned `NO_VISUAL_CONCEPT`: the charged rule emphasized a slogan without visibly transforming, revealing, or making sense of a complex state. Most of the lower field was inactive. The restart must preserve confidence while adding a visual verb that works with the words hidden.

### Fixed communication contract

- **Audience tension:** complexity cannot simply be erased; leaders need a way to inspect it, trace a relevant path, and form an answerable question.
- **Two-second takeaway:** the complex system remains intact, but a precise view makes one consequential path legible and traceable.
- **Capability truth:** AURENDOR makes complexity navigable and accountable; it does not promise a single magical answer or remove uncertainty.
- **Anthropomorphism:** level `0`.

### Route 12-A — The Answer Plane — **SELECTED**

- **Visual family / material:** conceptual spatial reveal; deep dark lattice of machined lines and joints, one clear mineral-glass inspection plane, one locally activated green path.
- **Textless mechanism:** outside the plane, the lattice remains dense, layered, and difficult to read. Where the plane intersects it, depth collapses into a precise cross-section: one continuous path and its evidence junctions become aligned and visible. The plane does not simplify or delete the surrounding system; it makes one question answerable from a deliberate view.
- **Static story:** foreground contains unresolved crossings; the action plane is the angled inspection plane; background preserves the full network. One selected path becomes legible only at the plane, and its source junctions remain visible rather than collapsing into a glowing answer orb.
- **Asset plan:** construct the lattice, inspection plane, intersections, and selected path as native 3D, SVG, or a controlled procedural scene. Do not use image generation for topology, text, nodes, or precision occlusion. If a generated texture is used, restrict it to an unbranded mineral background and record provenance. Every visible junction on the selected path must connect logically.
- **Crop / depth:** use an oblique close view with the inspection plane cutting from lower left toward upper right. The plane occupies roughly `26–32%` of the canvas and breaks both side edges. The lattice fills the frame at different depths; the revealed path sits near the optical center and remains visible at mobile size. Preserve only one calm type bay, created by the plane itself—not by leaving half the poster empty.
- **Typography relationship:** place `MAKE COMPLEXITY` outside the plane in the unresolved field using a clear, restrained display treatment. Place `ANSWERABLE` on or immediately beyond the plane, aligned with the revealed path. Dh Ranclo may shape `ANSWERABLE` only; reduce cuts in the setup phrase and use no decorative metadata line. The type transition mirrors the spatial transition, but the visual must still work when all type is hidden.
- **Why this closes the gaps:** it supplies an observable verb—align and reveal—without implying that complexity disappears. It activates the whole canvas, ties the green state to evidence, and gives the slogan a physical consequence rather than another underline.
- **Primary risk:** the lattice can become random-node technology decoration. Every non-selected line must participate in the same coherent structure, and the inspection plane must reveal a path through occlusion and alignment—not merely recolor nearby geometry.

### Route 12-B — Calibrated Core Sample

- **Visual family / material:** macro material transformation; layered mineral block, precision core aperture, one extracted cylinder retaining visible strata.
- **Textless mechanism:** a seemingly chaotic layered block is sampled by a calibrated aperture. The extracted core preserves every layer in ordered cross-section, turning a huge volume into one inspectable, answerable unit without pretending the original complexity vanished.
- **Asset plan:** original practical sculpture or physically precise 3D with credible strata, bore edges, debris control, and contact shadows. No planet, brain, database, or magic beam associations.
- **Crop:** the block fills the lower-left; the intact core rises diagonally into a right-side type field. The bore and core must both remain visible at mobile size.
- **Typography relationship:** the setup sits on the large block; `ANSWERABLE` aligns with the core's exposed strata.
- **Why it could work:** this is a strong material transformation and an immediately understandable inspection metaphor.
- **Why it is not selected:** extraction can imply reductionism or a single sample standing for the whole system; it is also materially close to other macro-object posts in the feed.

### Route 12-C — Answerability Gate Map

- **Visual family / material:** evidence-led operating diagram; native vector topology, hard-edged gates for source, owner, boundary, and decision.
- **Textless mechanism:** many paths enter, but only one can exit because it is the only route that physically closes all four required gates. Incomplete routes terminate visibly; the complete route becomes a single answerable unit.
- **Asset plan:** native vector only, with geometry—not labels or color alone—distinguishing complete and incomplete gates. Optional labels are added after the textless mechanism passes.
- **Crop:** an asymmetric map occupies the upper-left and center; the successful route exits toward the lower-right and creates the type counterfield.
- **Typography relationship:** the headline rides the exit direction and never sits in a generic poster header.
- **Why it could work:** it makes governance, evidence, and capability boundaries explicit and is easy to inspect technically.
- **Why it is not selected:** it risks repeating the benchmark's flat-diagram language and may remain too procedural for a brand-building breath.

### Route 12-D — Alignment Room

- **Visual family / material:** human-scale architectural scene; suspended translucent panels, etched fragments, one precise viewing threshold.
- **Textless mechanism:** from most of the room, fragments appear unrelated. From one marked threshold, the panels align into a coherent continuous route while still revealing the depth and plurality of the system.
- **Asset plan:** original architectural 3D scene with exact perspective, panel suspension, refraction, shadows, and scale cues. No person is required; a simple floor threshold establishes the point of view.
- **Crop:** wide-angle portrait from just outside the threshold; foreground panels crop aggressively, aligned route resolves at center depth, quiet type plane sits on the near wall.
- **Typography relationship:** the setup remains outside the aligned view; `ANSWERABLE` appears only within the resolved sightline.
- **Why it could work:** it is immersive, premium, and expresses perspective as a strategic act.
- **Why it is not selected:** execution cost and perspective risk are substantially higher, and the architectural scale may overwhelm a concise social post.

### Selected-route acceptance test

At mobile size with copy, green, and logo removed, reviewers must perceive a dense three-dimensional system that becomes one traceable path only where a precise plane intersects it. If they describe a glowing network, random nodes, futuristic interface, or decorative glass panel, route 12-A fails.

## Selection summary

| Brief | Selected route | Primary visual verb | Why it advances |
|---|---|---|---|
| `03` | Custody Baton | transfer → verify → receive → stop | Converts a passive object family into one bounded, interruptible action without faces or autonomy theatre |
| `05` | Accountability Press | loop → commit ownership → expose next action | Makes status chasing, ownership change, and forward action physical in one frame rather than two diagrams |
| `07` | Provenance Spine | bind → carry together | Introduces an actual owned evidence artifact and makes provenance inseparable from the decision |
| `12` | The Answer Plane | align → reveal | Adds a textless visual concept while truthfully preserving complexity and uncertainty |

## Handoff requirements before rendering

For each selected route, production must record:

1. a copy-free black-and-white mechanism sketch at mobile size;
2. the two-second textless-test result and failure descriptions;
3. a source/capability/provenance statement;
4. exact asset construction method and rights state;
5. crop, focal box, action plane, and negative-space job;
6. the selected type roles and verified font files;
7. a no-logo brand-recognition rationale;
8. the forbidden prior composition and forbidden category clichés;
9. original and mobile pixel renders with new SHA-256 values;
10. a new `3/9/12` feed simulation before any professional score is assigned.

No selected route is professional, approved, or golden until its final current pixels pass every required independent critic and comparison gate.
