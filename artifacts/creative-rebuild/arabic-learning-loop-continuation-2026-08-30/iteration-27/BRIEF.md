# Iteration 27 — Record Observation, Not Assumption

## Objective

Move the feed into customer/user listening with one practical research behavior: record what actually happened in a session before interpreting it.

## Evidence scope

The GOV.UK Service Manual advises researchers to observe how people perform tasks and what barriers they face, and to keep notes based on what was seen or heard rather than personal interpretation. It also recommends recording things people do, how they think, and how they feel. The post compresses this into a note-taking discipline; it does not claim that observation alone replaces interviews, consent, diverse recruitment, analysis, or triangulation.

## Research ethics boundary

The post is about observation quality, not permission to record people. Actual research still requires informed consent, privacy protection, secure handling, appropriate participants, and analysis beyond one observation.
