# Iteration 25 — Progress Is Real and Unfinished

## Objective

Create a nuanced Iraq-specific human-development post that acknowledges progress and remaining inequality without turning one national index category into triumphalism or a client claim.

## Exact copy

- Metadata: `من العراق · ٣٧`
- Headline: `التقدّم حقيقي` / `لكنه غير مكتمل`
- Support: `دخل العراق فئة التنمية البشرية المرتفعة في ٢٠٢٤، مع استمرار الفجوات بين المحافظات وبين النساء والرجال.`
- Source: `المصدر: التقرير الوطني للتنمية البشرية في العراق ٢٠٢٥.`

## Evidence scope

The Government of Iraq and UNDP launch release states that Iraq's Human Development Index reached 0.712 in 2024 and entered the high-human-development category for the first time, while progress remains uneven across governorates and between men and women. The post retains the category, year, and uneven-progress caveat without showing the number, implying causality, or projecting a future result.

## Image status

The background is an original, textless, AI-generated documentary-style illustration. All people are fictional and are not report participants, beneficiaries, public figures, or evidence of the claim. Exact Arabic and attribution are rendered separately in Chromium.
