# Iteration 33 — Service Continuity During an Outage

## Objective

Turn one Iraq-specific infrastructure condition into a service-design question: when electricity is interrupted, what part of the service can still continue?

## Exact copy

- Metadata: `استمرارية خدمة · ٤٥`
- Headline: `حين تنقطع الكهرباء`
- Interrupted display word: `انقطاع`
- Question: `هل تكمل خدمتك؟`
- Support: `في مسح منشآت العراق ٢٠٢٢، أفادت ٤١.٣٪ من منشآت القطاع الخاص الرسمية بتعرّضها لانقطاعات كهربائية.`
- Source: `المصدر: مسح منشآت العراق، مجموعة البنك الدولي، ٢٠٢٢.`

## Evidence scope

The World Bank Enterprise Survey's Iraq 2022 profile reports that 41.3% of surveyed formal private-sector firms experienced electrical outages. The survey interviewed owners or senior managers at 1,019 firms from December 2021 through August 2022 and is designed to represent the non-agricultural private economy covered by the Enterprise Survey. The post preserves the formal-firm population and survey year in the pixels.

The question about service continuity is a design prompt derived from the operating condition, not a survey finding. The source does not say that a particular service failed, prescribe an offline mode, or measure customer harm.

## Concept tournament

1. **Selected — interrupted type, continuous rail:** one large Arabic `انقطاع` is divided into displaced horizontal slices while a thin green line remains perfectly continuous beneath it.
2. Rejected — lightning bolt or socket: literal, generic, and unable to communicate service continuity.
3. Rejected — generator photograph: would imply one prescribed solution and add an invented Iraqi setting.
4. Rejected — giant `٤١.٣٪`: would repeat statistic-led feed behavior and overpower the design question.
5. Rejected — outage-frequency chart: would require additional denominator and duration explanation and drift toward a dashboard.

## Composition constraints

- 1080×1350, Arabic-only visible copy, RTL, 68 px text safe margin.
- Dark full-canvas field with pale live headline, one fragmented live word, a continuous AURENDOR-green rail, and a stable bright question.
- Only the metaphorical display word is disrupted; headline, question, evidence, source, and logo remain fully readable.
- `٤١.٣٪` remains inside a complete evidence sentence rather than becoming a hero.
- Support and source remain readable at 324×405.
- No socket, plug, lightning bolt, generator, battery, phone, app screen, outage map, chart, dashboard, photograph, generated image, or external visual asset.

## Professional design principles studied

- A flexible editorial grid can use a deliberate interruption to change rhythm while preserving the system around it.
- A second visual layer can create interference without making the underlying sentence unreadable.
- Typographic interruption can scale as a campaign mechanism when the core message remains stable.

References were used for principle study only. No composition, type treatment, crop, palette, line, grid, object, photograph, or project pixel was reused.

## Evidence boundary

The 41.3% estimate is not a current real-time rate and does not represent informal microbusinesses, agriculture, every region, or every establishment type. Experiencing an outage does not itself establish outage duration, service failure, customer loss, or which resilience measure is appropriate. Continuity decisions require service-specific research into channel, power, connectivity, safety, data, and support dependencies.
