# Iteration 31 — Digital Payment Needs Acceptance

## Objective

Translate one Iraq-specific commercial barrier into a direct Arabic post: digital payment tools need accessible merchant/service-provider acceptance points.

## Exact copy

- Metadata: `دفع رقمي · ٤٣`
- Headline: `الدفع الرقمي` / `لا يعمل وحده`
- Continuation: `يحتاج نقطة قبول`
- Support: `تصف الاستراتيجية الوطنية للشمول المالي نقص أجهزة الدفع لدى مقدّمي الخدمات بأنه العائق الرئيس للمدفوعات غير النقدية.`
- Source: `المصدر: البنك المركزي العراقي، ٢٠٢٥.`

## Evidence scope

The Central Bank of Iraq's National Financial Inclusion Strategy 2025–2029 reports that cash remains dominant and identifies lack of payment equipment among service providers as the main barrier to non-cash payments, followed by fees. The strategy draws this section from the national financial-inclusion diagnostic and World Bank analysis. The post states that barrier only; it does not claim that one terminal guarantees trust, adoption, inclusion, or sales.

## Concept tournament

1. **Selected — missing acceptance terminal:** an original terminal silhouette is cut from a dark field and surrounded by a bright dashed absence contour; the missing acceptance point is the visual subject.
2. Rejected — giant `٩٥٪` cash statistic: repeats the feed's survey-number behavior and shifts attention away from an actionable barrier.
3. Rejected — merchant photograph: would require invented context and risk stereotyping Iraqi retail.
4. Rejected — payment-app screen: a simulated interface cannot represent merchant acceptance infrastructure.

## Composition constraints

- 1080×1350, Arabic-only visible copy, RTL, 68 px text safe margin.
- Pale headline/source zones with one full-width dark acceptance field.
- Original SVG terminal only; no card brand, network logo, merchant name, receipt, currency, or fake transaction.
- Support and source remain readable at 324×405.
- No statistic as hero, photograph, payment brand, card network, interface, dashboard, external asset, generated image, or professional reference pixel.

## Evidence boundary

Acceptance equipment is one barrier in an ecosystem that also includes fees, trust, interoperability, electricity, awareness, consumer protection, and financial/digital literacy. The source does not establish a causal uplift from terminal deployment or describe every provider, region, or customer segment identically.

## Quality target

All critics ≥145/160; official-source fidelity, Arabic, mobile, originality, Iraq relevance, complete-feed, and evidence-boundary gates required; production disabled.
