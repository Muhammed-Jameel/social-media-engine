# Iteration 26 — Accuracy Is Not the Only Criterion

## Objective

Expand the feed into responsible AI with a practical, source-grounded reminder that technical accuracy alone does not establish trustworthiness.

## Exact copy

- Metadata: `ذكاء مسؤول · ٣٨`
- Headline: `الدقة ليست` / `المعيار الوحيد`
- Annotation column: `عدالة` / `خصوصية` / `مساءلة`
- Support: `قيّم العدالة والخصوصية والمساءلة إلى جانب الأداء التقني.`
- Reference: `مرجع: إطار «نيست» لإدارة مخاطر الذكاء الاصطناعي، وتوصية اليونسكو لأخلاقياته.`

## Evidence scope

NIST's AI RMF identifies validity/reliability alongside fairness with harmful bias managed, privacy enhancement, accountability/transparency, safety, security/resilience, and explainability/interpretability. UNESCO's AI ethics recommendation also centers fairness, privacy/data protection, responsibility/accountability, transparency/explainability, and human oversight. The post selects three non-performance dimensions as a reminder, not a complete framework or compliance test.

## Concept tournament

1. **Selected — bright annotation column:** a neon paper field carries the headline while a dark margin gives fairness, privacy, and accountability equal visual weight.
2. Rejected — robot or human/robot handshake: generic AI cliché and irrelevant to the risk-management message.
3. Rejected — brain/circuit illustration: decorative technology symbolism with no ethical specificity.
4. Rejected — framework checklist: would imply completeness, certification, or a compliance interface.

## Composition constraints

- 1080×1350, Arabic-only visible copy, RTL.
- Neon field with a fixed dark left annotation column; no source footer stack.
- Headline remains the focal point; the three ethical dimensions are independent annotations, not icons or scores.
- Reference remains readable at 324×405.
- No robot, brain, circuitry, dashboard, risk meter, shield, badge, imported asset, or generated image.

## Quality target

All critics ≥145/160; framework fidelity, Arabic, source legibility, contrast, mobile, originality, non-compliance framing, and feed gates required; production disabled.
