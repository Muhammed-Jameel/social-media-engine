# Iteration 32 — Account Open Does Not Mean Used

## Objective

Separate financial access from active use with one Iraq-specific Arabic post. The account is the entry point; activity is the behavior that still has to happen.

## Exact copy

- Metadata: `استخدام مالي · ٤٤`
- Headline: `فتح الحساب` / `لا يعني استخدامه`
- Support: `في التشخيص الوطني، أفاد ٥٤٪ من أصحاب الحسابات بعدم إجراء معاملة خلال الشهر السابق.`
- Source: `المصدر: الاستراتيجية الوطنية للشمول المالي، البنك المركزي العراقي، ٢٠٢٥.`

## Evidence scope

The Central Bank of Iraq's National Financial Inclusion Strategy 2025–2029 summarizes a national diagnostic in which 54% of account holders reported no transaction during the previous month. The post states that access and recent activity are different conditions. It does not describe a current real-time KPI, claim that an account was closed or blocked, identify a cause, or infer business impact.

## Concept tournament

1. **Selected — circulation loop with a stationary sphere:** an original transparent loop implies a path while one still sphere shows that access to a path does not prove movement through it.
2. Rejected — giant `٥٤٪`: would repeat the feed's statistic-led behavior and make the diagnostic more universal than its scope supports.
3. Rejected — unused bank card: would narrow account activity to card use and introduce a financial product that the source does not specify.
4. Rejected — blank transaction ledger: would collide with Iteration 29's ledger-derived completion-rate system.

## Composition constraints

- 1080×1350, Arabic-only visible copy, RTL, 68 px text safe margin.
- Full-bleed original textless still life with live Ghroob Arabic and canonical horizontal AURENDOR logo.
- The evidence sentence and `٥٤٪` remain support, never the visual hero.
- One transparent loop, one motionless deep-green sphere, pale mineral surface, spatial quiet, and restrained refraction.
- No account screen, bank card, cash, phone, wallet, transaction UI, brand, barrier icon, or invented Iraqi setting.
- Support and source remain readable at 324×405.

## Image provenance

The source still life was generated with the built-in image-generation system from a new text prompt, without any reference image. The requested scene contained one transparent glass loop and one motionless green sphere on an off-white mineral surface, with no text, number, logo, UI, card, cash, phone, people, or financial brand. Arabic copy, source line, and logo were added later as live HTML/CSS in the renderer. No professional-reference pixel was supplied to generation.

## Evidence boundary

The diagnostic does not establish why an account holder made no transaction. Possible factors discussed by the strategy include cash preference, cost, trust, access, awareness, protection, and infrastructure, but this single metric cannot attribute cause. One inactive month also does not prove permanent inactivity or account failure.

## Professional design principles studied

- Conceptual still life can turn ordinary form, texture, and spatial balance into a visual narrative.
- Reflective glass and controlled light can create depth without adding literal product imagery.
- Spatial silence and imperfect balance can hold calm tension around one symbolic condition.

References were used for principle study only. No composition, object arrangement, typography, palette, crop, texture, photograph, or project pixel was reused.
