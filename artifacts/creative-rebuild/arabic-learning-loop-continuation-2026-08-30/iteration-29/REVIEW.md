## Why it passes

- `البدء لا يكفي` reframes a service visit as the beginning of measurement rather than success itself.
- The design visualizes an exact definition without inventing a chart, result, benchmark, or percentage.
- The 250 px mathematical rail gives division and equality their own visual role while the Arabic fraction remains the information hierarchy.
- The pale ledger field and asymmetric rail add a new feed silhouette without imitating a dashboard or receipt.
- At 324×405, headline is approximately 29.4 px, numerator 18.3 px, denominator 16.5 px, result 31.2 px, support 12.6 px, and reference 10.2 px.

## Metric audit

- Numerator: completed transactions — exact to the source.
- Denominator: all transactions started, which includes partial and failed transactions — exact to the source at the post's level of detail.
- Start/end requirement: stated in the support.
- No target, trend, causal claim, service result, comparison, or AURENDOR/client performance claim appears.

## Arabic audit

- Arabic-only visible copy, RTL order, `حدّد` shadda, `بدايةً` and `نهايةً` tanwīn, hamza forms, punctuation, fraction reading order, and line breaks: pass.
- `نسبة من أكملوا المهمة إلى كل من بدأوها` reads as numerator-to-denominator rather than implying absolute completion volume.

## Hard-fail audit

- Exact copy, Ghroob regular/bold, canvas bounds, contrast, logo, original/mobile dimensions, hashes, and reference legibility: pass.
- No percentage invention, funnel, dashboard, progress bar, fake receipt, interface, external visual asset, generated image, or professional reference pixel: pass.
- Production publication: disabled.

## Residual risk

Completion rate is not overall service success. Production use must define genuine start/end events, handle save-and-return and assisted journeys, exclude bots/internal/test traffic, distinguish completion from approval, and interpret the metric with satisfaction, cost, digital take-up, segmentation, and research.
