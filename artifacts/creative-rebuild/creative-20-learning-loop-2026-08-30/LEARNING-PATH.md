# AURENDOR Creative 20 — Learning Path

## Mission

Produce twenty Arabic, mobile-legible, professionally finished social posts whose visual mechanisms are meaning-bearing rather than decorative. A post is counted only after it clears every release gate; weak first passes are rejected and rebuilt.

## Brand and format freeze

- Canvas: 1080×1350 (4:5), with a 324×405 mobile-review render.
- Safe area: 68–72 px production margin; no live text outside the safe area.
- Direction: Arabic-only, RTL, horizontal Ghroob Arabic text, no Arabic tracking.
- Palette: deep `#003F35`, deep-2 `#00302A`, pale `#77FF70`, neon `#0EDB23`, paper `#F4F8F5`, ink `#0B201B`.
- Accent budget: neon at or below 10% of the canvas.
- Branding: canonical horizontal AURENDOR mark, small and isolated from the concept field.
- Publication: disabled. This cohort is a reviewed artifact set, not a publishing action.

## What the research changes

1. **Arabic is a contextual script.** Joining behavior, dots, marks, and ligatures must be evaluated as part of the image—not treated as a Latin-shaped text box. This comes from Google Design's Arabic type work and Adobe's Middle Eastern typography guidance.
2. **Exclusion zones are optical, not merely numeric.** Pentagram's Sharjah work treats Arabic proportions and exclusion zones as first-class identity decisions. Every text/shape relationship therefore gets a visible quiet zone.
3. **Type carries the idea.** The strongest references use one focal read, then a second meaning revealed by material, cropping, transformation, or sequence. The cohort avoids a headline pasted above a generic icon.
4. **A campaign needs unity without repetition.** Logo, type, palette, and spacing remain stable; the semantic mechanism changes in every post.

## Design Asset Plan

1. Asset type: canonical AURENDOR logo and Ghroob Arabic fonts.
   Source: project files in `apps/web/public/brand` and `apps/web/src/app/fonts`.
   Why: exact brand fidelity and reliable Arabic shaping.
   License caution: internal licensed brand assets; do not redistribute outside approved AURENDOR outputs.
   Use: small lower-left logo and all visible Arabic copy.
2. Asset type: original deterministic SVG/CSS material systems.
   Source: authored inside the renderer; no external pixels.
   Why: twenty genuinely distinct mechanisms without copied campaigns or stock-template residue.
   License caution: original project output.
   Use: paper cuts, folds, architectural voids, typographic masks, relief, layers, and shadows.
3. Asset type: professional references used as design data only.
   Source: Google Design, Adobe, Pentagram, W3C and the existing curated professional-reference dataset.
   Why: typography, exclusion-zone, hierarchy, and Arabic-layout principles.
   License caution: no source image is downloaded, traced, embedded, or supplied to generation.
   Use: principles and critique criteria only.

## Loop

1. Generate a twenty-concept slate with no repeated visual mechanism.
2. Render production and mobile files with live Arabic text.
3. Run technical preflight: exact copy, RTL, font load, bounds, dimensions, hashes.
4. Review the mobile contact sheet before the full-size sheet.
5. Score each post blindly on concept, stopping power, composition, Arabic typography, geometry clearance, material finish, brand fit, originality, and mobile reading.
6. Reject any post with a hard failure or a score below 88/100. Creativity and Arabic geometry must each score at least 9/10.
7. Rebuild rejected posts using a different mechanism—not small cosmetic edits.
8. Repeat until twenty posts pass, then freeze hashes and assemble the final feed.
