# Creative 20 — Loop Review

## Outcome

Twenty of twenty Arabic posts clear the defined release floor. They are creative-review artifacts only; publication remains disabled.

## Pass 01

- Rendered: 20
- Technical passes: 20
- Visual passes: 14
- Rejected: 6 (`04`, `05`, `06`, `07`, `10`, `15`)

### Why the six were rejected

- `04`: translucent panel rather than a convincing veil.
- `05`: stacked document pages made the design feel like a report cover.
- `06`: the diagonal plane was dramatic but semantically ambiguous.
- `07`: the operational cut-section looked like a conventional UI diagram.
- `10`: three process bars reproduced a familiar flowchart rhythm.
- `15`: two stacked documents read as cards rather than decision memory.

## Pass 02 rebuilds

- `04`: rebuilt as an irregular membrane peeled from a bright bounded core.
- `05`: rebuilt as a signal eroding into dated sediment before the decision edge.
- `06`: rebuilt as one fraying support carrying the full decision mass.
- `07`: rebuilt as a pristine facade physically lifted to reveal an operational weave.
- `10`: rebuilt as a redundant material loop cut away to expose the direct route.
- `15`: rebuilt as a result layer with a precise window preserving the earlier reason.

## Final gate

- Required total: 88/100 or higher.
- Required creativity: 9/10 or higher.
- Required Arabic geometry: 9/10 or higher.
- Hard failures: incorrect Arabic/RTL, text-shape collision, hidden dots or marks, unsafe crop, wrong dimensions, missing logo, generic stock/reference pixels, invented proof, or publication eligibility.
- Result: 20 pass, 0 rebuild, 0 retire, 0 publication eligible.

## Cohort character

The constant campaign grammar is the AURENDOR palette, Ghroob typography, small canonical logo, assertive RTL hierarchy, and controlled negative space. Variety comes from twenty different semantic mechanisms rather than twenty templates with changed headlines.
