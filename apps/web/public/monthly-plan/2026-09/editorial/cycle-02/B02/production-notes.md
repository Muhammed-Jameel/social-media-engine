# B02 — A finishing change has two consequences

Six 1080×1350 carousel PNGs, a 1080×1920 supporting Story, a six-page LinkedIn PDF, five platform captions, editable HTML/CSS/SVG, original asset files and 360px mobile proofs. These assets are rendered for review; nothing is published or scheduled.

## Editorial and creative argument

A tactile material-selection board makes a finishing substitution concrete. The first two slides move from a buyer’s familiar request to two separate consequences: two million Iraqi dinars more and four extra days of supplier lead time. These are invented teaching values, visibly labelled, not supplier prices or market data. Two authored timeline cases then distinguish parallel work from a dependent activity waiting for material. They are qualitative diagrams, not a computed project schedule.

The fourth slide separates a request from a documented decision and provides a manual administrative template in the public caption. The fifth uses an actual Bunyan change-request card; the sixth invites a product demonstration using the reader’s own way of working. No free trial or availability promise is made.
