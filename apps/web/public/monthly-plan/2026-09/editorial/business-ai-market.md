# Iraqi business needs → SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN AI services editorial programme

Research cutoff: 10 September 2026. This is a desk study for editorial planning, not an Iraqi customer interview study or a quantified demand forecast. The completed output is ten Arabic briefs in `pillars/services.json`, including seven carousels and three scripts; no videos are being produced in this phase.

## What the local evidence actually tells us

The Iraqi private sector should not be treated as one interchangeable buyer. The [World Bank enterprise survey panel catalogue](https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/8156) covers eligible formal private establishments with at least five employees and includes manufacturing and selected service sectors. Its management, finance, infrastructure and supply questions describe different constraints. The catalogue was created in January 2026, but its latest survey wave concerns 2022; it is not a 2026 adoption survey. Its panel metadata and the separate standalone catalogue have different fieldwork descriptions, so no fieldwork date or national prevalence statistic is used in social copy.

The [UNDP/Ministry of Planning focus-group report](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2024-10/undp_iq_fgd_final_report_english.pdf), covering Kirkuk, Babil and Dhi Qar, provides more useful qualitative direction. Its cover says December 2023; the publication page is dated 10 October 2024. Participants described limited experience with technology and English, while recommendations included practical management, customer communication and market-research skills. Financing and internet constraints also appear. These are reported perceptions and proposals in three governorates, not percentages describing every Iraqi business. The policy context cannot prove demand for a specific SOCIAL_MEDIA_PLUGIN service.

The editorial implication is to explain a recognisable piece of work before naming a technology. A distributor may care about an incomplete supplier offer. A service company may care about handing an inquiry to the right employee. Both benefit from a reusable worksheet even if they never commission software. Electricity, bank lending conditions and macroeconomic uncertainty remain real constraints; a text model does not remove them. We therefore avoid “AI fixes Iraqi business” rhetoric and keep each proposed intervention inside the information task it can plausibly support.

## Where AI fits, and what must be designed around it

The first useful distinction is between understanding an input and making a business commitment. A model can be asked to identify missing service-request fields or prepare a bilingual clarification. The firm still needs an authoritative source, a recipient and an acceptance rule. [Anthropic’s engineering guidance](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents) distinguishes fixed workflows from systems that dynamically choose their steps and recommends starting with sufficient simplicity. Its routing and chaining patterns inform our diagrams, but no vendor-specific model or mutable framework is prescribed.

This produces S01: choose a first task by input availability, repeatability and the ability to judge its output. S10 completes that journey with a different deliverable: a worked contract-renewal handoff specification, including the trigger, sources, owner, exception and record of the previous action. Selection and implementation are deliberately separate lessons. The reader can use either with an internal colleague or another supplier.

S02 turns language support into a purchasing action. The fictional supplier wording contains an undefined confirmation trigger and an absent warranty policy. Translation alone does not complete the commercial information. The reusable output keeps the original wording next to its Arabic meaning and generates precise questions in both languages. We do not interpret legal obligations or recommend accepting an offer; the observable benefit is a better clarification request before approval.

S03 separates customer questions by what is needed to resolve them: an approved information source, an order identifier plus a current record, or an exception owner. Its difficult example is a mixed message containing both a price question and a complaint. This avoids the shallow claim that a company needs a bot simply because it receives messages. A manager can test the proposed categories manually using earlier messages before connecting any system.

## Knowledge and documents are operating assets

Internal knowledge is useful when someone can find a current instruction and understand its scope. [Anthropic’s retrieval research](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/contextual-retrieval) explains how retrieving business-specific material and preserving its context can help generation; exact identifiers may also require lexical matching. S04 converts that principle into a service-procedure card with an owner, version, effective date and exceptions. The employee-facing output includes a paragraph reference. Permissions and document approval are additional design requirements, not capabilities attributed to retrieval alone.

For document processing, S05 begins with the employee’s next action rather than an impressive scan animation. The useful artifact contains selected fields, their locations in the original document and a discrepancy queue. [Microsoft’s documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/document-intelligence/concept/accuracy-confidence?view=doc-intel-4.0.0) distinguishes word and field confidence and discusses variation between document formats. That supports testing actual input diversity. We do not publish a universal confidence threshold or imply that a correctly recognised number constitutes accounting approval. Separate currencies and possible duplicate invoices make the business consequence concrete.

S06 applies the same attention to context to receivables correspondence. Its card includes the ledger update time, last promise and unresolved objection. The model’s proposed role is preparing a summary and draft; the accounting owner confirms the balance and the relationship owner decides the action. This is not an assertion that AI improves liquidity. It is a narrow administrative workflow whose preparation time and rework can be measured.

## Better decisions require better definitions

Demand planning supplies a useful counterintuitive lesson for distributors. [Amazon’s researchers describe stockout flags among forecasting inputs](https://www.amazon.science/blog/data-on-correlated-products-and-sellers-helps-improve-demand-forecasting), while the [FreshRetailNet research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.16319) explicitly addresses censored sales during stockouts. Its first submission was 22 May 2025, with the consulted fifth version dated 18 June 2026. Neither source establishes performance for an Iraqi wholesaler.

S07 uses three entirely fictional days to distinguish zero recorded sales from unknown demand during unavailability. It provides an availability/returns/price/context table and asks the reader to audit one SKU. It intentionally does not impute the lost demand, invent a forecast or claim that a language-model chat should be the forecasting engine. The commercial opportunity is a properly defined decision-support project after data preparation, not a magic prediction.

## Training and measurement connect the programme

The [November 2023 NBER working-paper version of Generative AI at Work](https://www.nber.org/papers/w31161) studied assistance across 5,179 support agents and reported heterogeneous effects, with larger gains among less experienced workers. The [original researchers’ HBS account of the 758-consultant experiment](https://aiinstitute.hbs.edu/navigating-the-jagged-technological-frontier/) also ties usefulness to task suitability. These findings support role-specific trials, not copying a percentage into an Iraqi sales claim. Later published versions must not be mixed with this working-paper version.

S08 consequently provides a proposed thirty-minute training exercise: define a deliverable, create it with AI, and have a colleague accept or return it. Its timing is an authored workshop suggestion. S09 gives a measurement card that includes review and correction. The arithmetic example explicitly shows why a two-minute draft plus eight-minute review is ten minutes of work, not a ten-minute saving against twelve minutes. Accepted output, rework, operating cost and comparable case types define a more meaningful trial.

The resulting sequence moves from selecting a task, through concrete departmental artifacts, to a training rehearsal, a measurement rule and an implementation brief. Nine primary CTAs ask the reader to do something useful; only S10 directly invites an inquiry. Service bridges remain modest statements of authorised scope. All examples are authored and labelled. Local customer interviews and controlled pilots would be needed to rank these needs by incidence or establish ROI; neither has been invented for this content plan.
