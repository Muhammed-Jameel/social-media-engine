Status: new creative direction for review. Earlier P04/P08 cuts were rejected by the user. This pilot is not publication approval and does not change scheduling.

The takeaway is specific: 65% stage progress and two unfinished checklist items describe different aspects of the same project update. The daily report explains the pump delay. The example uses real application pixels with synthetic demonstration data; it makes no client-performance or AI-feature claim.
