# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a welcoming environment for all contributors.

We pledge to make participation in this project harassment-free and respectful, independent of:

- age
- disability
- ethnicity
- gender identity and expression
- nationality
- religion
- sex
- sexual orientation
- experience level

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and respectful.
- Keep feedback focused on code and evidence.
- Respect safety boundaries and production gating.
- Report unsafe behavior and security concerns promptly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks
- Harassment or discriminatory conduct
- Publicly sharing secrets or private credentials
- Malicious or reckless actions that impact test environments

## Enforcement

Violations can lead to content removal, temporary restrictions, or permanent ban from participation, depending on severity.
